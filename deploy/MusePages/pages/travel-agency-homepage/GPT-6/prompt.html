Prompt

Create an immersive, inspiring homepage for a travel agency called "Wanderly" that specializes in curated vacation packages, adventure travel, and luxury getaways. The design should evoke wanderlust, feel premium yet approachable, and drive trip inquiries.

1. NAVIGATION BAR
- Logo: "Wanderly" with a compass rose icon
- Links: Destinations, Trip Styles, Deals, About Us, Blog
- Right side: phone number "(555) 290-8700", heart/wishlist icon, "Plan My Trip" CTA button (solid #0D9488 teal)
- Transparent over hero image, transitions to white with shadow on scroll
- Hamburger menu on mobile

2. HERO SECTION (full-viewport height)
- Full-bleed background: stunning aerial photo of turquoise ocean water meeting white sand beach
- Centered overlay content:
  - Tagline: "Your Next Adventure Starts Here"
  - Headline: "Curated Travel Experiences Around the World"
- TRIP SEARCH BAR (floating card centered on hero, white background, rounded, prominent shadow):
  - 4 fields in a row: Destination (dropdown: "Where to?"), Travel Dates (date range picker), Travelers (number stepper "2 Adults"), Trip Style (dropdown: Beach, Adventure, Cultural, Luxury, Family)
  - Large "Search Trips" button (solid teal #0D9488)
  - Below search bar: quick links — "🔥 Trending: Bali · Amalfi Coast · Kyoto · Patagonia · Iceland"
- Subtle parallax scroll on background image

- 4 columns: Destinations, Trip Styles, Company, Support
- Destinations: Greece, Japan, Costa Rica, Italy, Bali, Iceland, "All Destinations"
- Trip Styles: Beach, Adventure, Cultural, Luxury, Family, Food & Wine

Design: Immersive, aspirational, premium. Primary: #0D9488 (teal), secondary: #134E4A (dark teal), accent: #F59E0B (amber for deals/badges). Backgrounds: white with #F0FDFA teal-tinted sections and #F9FAFB neutral sections. Typography: DM Serif Display for headings (elegant serif), Inter for body. Cards with 16px border-radius, generous image areas, soft shadows. Lots of high-quality destination photography.

Responsive: On mobile, search bar fields stack vertically inside a collapsible panel. Destination cards become horizontally scrollable carousel. Trip style grid goes 2-column. Stats bar becomes 2x2 grid. Blog cards stack vertically. Navigation collapses to hamburger with drawer. Sticky bottom bar: "Plan My Trip" CTA.
