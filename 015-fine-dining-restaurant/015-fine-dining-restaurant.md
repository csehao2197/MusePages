Design: Luxurious, minimal, editorial. Color palette: black (#0F0F0F), cream (#FAF8F3), off-white (#F5F0E8), gold (#C9A84C). Typography: Cormorant Garamond for headings (elegant serif), Montserrat Light for body (clean, refined). No rounded corners on cards — use sharp edges for sophistication. Thin gold line dividers between sections. Large whitespace, large typography. All text centered unless in split layouts. Photos should feel warm, moody, and cinematic with shallow depth of field.

Responsive: On mobile, all split layouts stack vertically. Menu items remain centered with comfortable spacing. Gallery becomes 2-column. Navigation becomes full-screen overlay. Reservation widget fields stack. Wine bottles scroll horizontally. Press quotes become vertical stack. Font sizes scale down but remain generous. Bottom sticky bar with "Reserve" CTA on mobile.
