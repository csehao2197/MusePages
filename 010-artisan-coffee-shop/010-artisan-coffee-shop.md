Design: Warm, artisanal, coffee-forward. Primary: #92400E (warm amber-800 for CTAs and accents), secondary: #3C1A0B (dark roast brown for contrast sections), background alternating between white (#FFFFFF) and warm cream (#FFFBEB). Typography: Playfair Display for headlines (elegant serif), Inter for body text (clean and readable). Cards with 12px border-radius, subtle warm shadows. Photography-driven with rich, warm color grading. Generous whitespace. Coffee-stain watermark textures as subtle background accents where appropriate.

Responsive: Mobile-first. Hero image becomes portrait crop with centered text overlay. Drink cards and product cards become horizontal swipe carousels. Menu grid stacks to single column. Location cards stack vertically. Newsletter and loyalty sections stack. All touch targets minimum 44px. Navigation collapses to hamburger with slide-out drawer. Order Ahead CTA remains visible in mobile nav.
