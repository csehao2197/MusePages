Design: Minimal, image-forward, editorial. Primary: #0A0A0A (near black), secondary: #D4A853 (warm gold for accents), tertiary: #F5F5F4 (warm gray for alt sections). Backgrounds: white, near-black, and warm gray — no bright colors competing with photography. Typography: Instrument Serif for headings (refined editorial serif), Inter for body and meta text. Let photos dominate — minimal borders, no card shadows, thin dividers only. Full-bleed images wherever possible. Generous whitespace between sections. Cursor changes to zoom icon over gallery images.

Responsive: On mobile, hero post is full-viewport with stacked text. Editorial grid becomes single-column stacked cards (large photos). Destinations scroll horizontally with snap points. Gallery mosaic becomes 2-column grid. Gear reviews stack vertically. Before/after slider works with touch drag. Print cards become 2x2 grid. Newsletter section is full-bleed photo with stacked form. Navigation is logo + hamburger with dark overlay. No floating bars — keep the reading experience clean.
