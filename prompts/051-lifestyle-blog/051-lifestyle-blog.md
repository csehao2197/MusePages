Design: Warm, editorial, curated. Primary: #D946EF (fuchsia-500 for accents, CTAs, category pills), secondary: #1C1917 (stone-900 for text), accent: #F59E0B (amber for trending indicators, highlights). Backgrounds: white primary, #FAF5FF (lavender tint) for featured sections, #FDF4FF for collections. Typography: Playfair Display for headlines (elegant serif, editorial feel), Inter for body text and UI. Cards with 12px border-radius, subtle shadows. Photography-forward layout with generous white space. Grain texture on accent sections for tactile editorial feel. Overall mood: Sunday morning with a coffee — unhurried, intentional, beautiful.

Responsive: Mobile-first. Hero image reduces to 50vh. Editor's Picks becomes horizontal scroll. Article grid stacks to single column. Newsletter section stacks vertically. Trending strip scrolls horizontally. Instagram strip shows 4 images with scroll. Category pills scroll horizontally with hidden scrollbar. Sticky bottom bar with "Subscribe" CTA on mobile. All images lazy-loaded. Touch targets minimum 44px.
