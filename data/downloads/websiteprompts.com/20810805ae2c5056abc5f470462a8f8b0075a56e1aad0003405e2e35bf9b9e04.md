Create a product comparison landing page:

1. HERO SECTION
- "[Your Product] vs [Competitor]"
- "The better alternative to [Competitor]"
- Brief positioning statement
- CTA: "Switch to [Product]" or "Start Free Trial"

2. QUICK COMPARISON
Side by side at a glance:
- Your product vs Competitor
- 3-4 key differentiators
- Checkmarks and X marks
- Your product wins

3. DETAILED COMPARISON TABLE
Feature-by-feature:
| Feature | Your Product | Competitor |
| ------- | ------------ | ---------- |
| Feature 1 | ✓ | ✓ |
| Feature 2 | ✓ | Limited |
| Feature 3 | ✓ | ✗ |
| Price | $X | $XX |

Categories:
- Core features
- Integrations
- Support
- Pricing
- Security

4. WHY SWITCH
"Why [Product] is better"
- Key advantages (3-5)
- Each with explanation
- Supporting evidence

5. WHAT OTHERS SAY
- Reviews comparing both
- G2/Capterra ratings
- "After switching..." testimonials
- Third-party comparisons

6. MIGRATION SECTION
"Switching is easy"
- How to migrate
- Data import tools
- Dedicated support
- "We'll help you switch"

7. PRICE COMPARISON
- Side-by-side pricing
- Total cost of ownership
- Hidden costs they have
- Money you'll save

8. TESTIMONIALS
From people who switched:
- "We switched from [Competitor] because..."
- Before/after experience
- Time/money saved

9. FAQ
- How does [feature] compare?
- Can I import my data?
- What if I don't like it?
- Is there a free trial?

10. FINAL CTA
- Strong call to switch
- Free trial offer
- Migration assistance

Design: Fair but favorable comparison. Back up claims. Make switching feel safe and easy.
