Create a newsletter signup landing page:

1. HERO SECTION
- Compelling headline
  - "Join 10,000+ readers"
  - "Weekly insights on [topic]"
- Subheadline explaining value
- Email signup form (prominent)
- "Subscribe" button
- Privacy note: "No spam, unsubscribe anytime"
- Social proof: Subscriber count or testimonial

2. WHAT YOU'LL GET
Benefits section:
- 3-4 benefits with icons
  - Weekly curated content
  - Exclusive insights
  - Early access to content
  - Community access
- What topics are covered
- Frequency: "Every Tuesday"

3. SAMPLE CONTENT
"See what you'll receive"
- Preview of past newsletter
- Screenshot or embedded sample
- Links to past issues
- "View archive"

4. TESTIMONIALS
"What readers say"
- 3 reader testimonials
- Name, title, photo
- Short quote about value
- Social proof badges

5. ABOUT THE AUTHOR
- Photo
- Who writes it
- Credentials/authority
- Why they started it

6. FAQ
Common questions:
- How often will I receive emails?
- Can I unsubscribe?
- Is it really free?
- What email platform do you use?

7. FINAL CTA
- Repeat signup form
- Urgency or exclusivity if appropriate
- "Join now"

8. FOOTER
- Links to main blog
- Social media
- Privacy policy
- Contact

Design: Single focus - get signups. Remove distractions. Build trust. Mobile-optimized.
