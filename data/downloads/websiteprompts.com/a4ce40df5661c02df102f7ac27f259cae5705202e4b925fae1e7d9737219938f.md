Design: Urgent, exciting, conversion-driven. Primary: #F97316 (orange for CTAs), secondary: #0F172A (dark navy for headers/hero), accent: #EF4444 (red for urgency/savings). Backgrounds: white with alternating tinted sections (#FFFBEB warm, #EFF6FF blue). Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans or Outfit for headings, Inter for body. Cards with 16px border-radius, generous photos, prominent pricing. Countdown timers with monospaced digits. Savings badges always visible and prominent.

Responsive: Mobile-first. Flash deals become full-width swipeable carousel. Filter bar becomes expandable accordion. Deal cards stack to single column. Category icons become horizontal scroll. Price alert form stacks vertically. Sticky mobile bottom bar showing "47 Deals" count + "Filter" button.
