Design: Warm, artisan, rustic-modern. Primary: #B45309 (warm amber), secondary: #92400E (deep brown), cream background: #FFFBEB, card surfaces: #FEF3C7, body text: #451A03. Typography: Playfair Display for headings (serif, elegant), Inter for body text, and a script font (e.g., Dancing Script) for taglines. Cards with 16px border-radius and soft warm shadows. Paper-like texture on section backgrounds where appropriate.

Responsive: On mobile, all grids stack to single column. Menu tabs become horizontal scrollable pills. Gallery switches to 2-column grid. Carousel shows one review at a time with swipe. Navigation collapses to hamburger. Sticky bottom bar with "Order Now" CTA on mobile.
