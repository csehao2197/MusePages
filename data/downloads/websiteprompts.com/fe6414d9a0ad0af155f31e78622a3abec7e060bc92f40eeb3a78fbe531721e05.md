Create an agency contact page:

1. PAGE HEADER
- "Let's Talk" or "Get in Touch"
- Brief welcoming message
- "We'd love to hear about your project"

2. CONTACT FORM
Project inquiry form:

Basic fields:
- Full name *
- Email address *
- Phone number
- Company/Organization

Project details:
- Service interested in (dropdown/checkboxes)
  - Branding
  - Web Design
  - Development
  - Marketing
  - Other
- Project budget (dropdown)
  - Under $10K
  - $10K - $25K
  - $25K - $50K
  - $50K - $100K
  - $100K+
  - Not sure
- Timeline
  - ASAP
  - 1-3 months
  - 3-6 months
  - 6+ months
  - Flexible
- Project description (textarea)
- How did you hear about us? (dropdown)

File upload (optional):
- "Attach brief or RFP"

Submit button:
- "Send Message" or "Start the Conversation"

Privacy note:
- "We'll respond within 24 hours"

3. DIRECT CONTACT INFO
Alternative ways to reach:
- Email: hello@agency.com
- Phone: +1 (555) 123-4567
- Social: Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram

4. OFFICE LOCATIONS
For each office:
- City name
- Full address
- Phone number
- Email
- Map embed
- Hours of operation

Multiple offices:
- Tabs or cards for each
- Map with all locations

5. MEETING BOOKING
- "Schedule a call"
- Calendly or similar embed
- 15/30/60 min options

6. FAQ
Common questions:
- What's your typical process?
- How long does a project take?
- Do you work with startups?
- What's your minimum budget?
- Do you sign NDAs?

7. RESPONSE EXPECTATIONS
- "We typically respond within 24 hours"
- "For urgent inquiries, call us"
- Working hours

8. ADDITIONAL CTAs
- "Not ready to chat? Follow us"
- Social media links
- Newsletter signup
- Resources/blog

9. SUCCESS STATE
After form submission:
- Thank you message
- What happens next
- Timeline expectation
- Calendar booking prompt

Design: Welcoming, low-friction. Make it easy to reach out. Show availability.
