Design: Bold, dark, energetic. Background: #0F172A (slate-900) as primary, #1E293B (slate-800) for cards and sections. Accent: #F97316 (orange-500) for CTAs, highlights, countdown, and interactive elements. Text: white (#FFFFFF) for headings, #94A3B8 (slate-400) for body text. Typography: Oswald or Bebas Neue for headlines (bold, condensed, high-impact), Inter for body. Cards with 12px border-radius, subtle glowing borders on hover. Strong contrast throughout. Animated counters and progress bars for energy. No pastels — this is a high-intensity brand.

Responsive: Mobile-first. Hero countdown timer boxes shrink proportionally. Feature grid becomes single column. Workout day cards become horizontal swipe. Timeline becomes vertical.
