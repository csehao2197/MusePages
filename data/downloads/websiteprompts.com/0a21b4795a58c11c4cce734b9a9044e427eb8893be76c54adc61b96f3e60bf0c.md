Design: Clean, data-forward, scannable. Primary: #7C3AED (purple-600 for CTAs and accents), secondary: #1A1A2E (near-black for headings), body text: #475569 (slate-600). Success green: #10B981 for "Best" badges and pros. Error red: #EF4444 for cons. Backgrounds: white for comparison table rows, #F8FAFC for alternating rows. Typography: Inter for everything — weights 400 (body), 500 (specs), 600 (product names, prices), 700 (headings). Table cells vertically centered, generous padding (16px). Green highlight cells (#F0FDF4) for best-in-category values. Sticky product header with z-index layering for smooth scroll experience.

Responsive: Mobile-first. On screens under 768px, the comparison table switches to a horizontal-scroll layout with the first product column visible and swipe to see others. Sticky header shows 1 product at a time with left/right arrows to switch. "Best For" cards stack vertically. Pros/cons stack vertically. Add "Compare at a glance" toggle that shows only rows where products differ. Filter sidebar for mobile: chips to toggle which specs to show. All touch targets 44px minimum.
