DESIGN SYSTEM
- Primary: #E11D48 (rose-600) for CTAs, accents, and active states
- Secondary: #1E1E2E (dark slate) for headers and footer
- Body text: #334155 (slate-700) on white backgrounds
- Accent background: #FFF1F2 (rose-50) for highlighted sections
- Section backgrounds: #F8FAFC (cool slate) for alternating sections
- Typography: Montserrat for headings (bold, condensed), Inter for body
- Cards: 12px border-radius, subtle shadow (0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.06))
- Fluid motion aesthetic: use subtle curved dividers between sections (SVG wave shapes)
