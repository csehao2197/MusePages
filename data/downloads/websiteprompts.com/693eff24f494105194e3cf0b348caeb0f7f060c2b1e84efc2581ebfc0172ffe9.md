Create HTML email templates for a SaaS product:

1. SHARED EMAIL STRUCTURE
- Max width: 600px, centered
- Header: Logo
- Body: Content area
- Footer: Company info, unsubscribe link, social icons

2. WELCOME EMAIL
Subject: "Welcome to TaskFlow! Let's get started"

Content:
- "Hi [First Name],"
- "Welcome to TaskFlow! We're excited to have you."
- 3 quick start tips with icons
- Primary CTA: "Go to Dashboard"
- "Need help? Reply to this email or check our Help Center"

3. PASSWORD RESET EMAIL
Subject: "Reset your TaskFlow password"

Content:
- "Hi [First Name],"
- "We received a request to reset your password."
- "Click the button below to create a new password."
- Primary CTA: "Reset Password"
- "This link expires in 24 hours."
- "If you didn't request this, you can ignore this email."

4. TEAM INVITATION EMAIL
Subject: "[Inviter Name] invited you to join [Workspace] on TaskFlow"

Content:
- "[Inviter Name] has invited you to collaborate on TaskFlow."
- Workspace name and description
- Primary CTA: "Accept Invitation"
- "This invitation expires in 7 days."

5. WEEKLY DIGEST EMAIL
Subject: "Your TaskFlow weekly summary"

Content:
- "Here's what happened this week:"
- Stats: Tasks completed, Projects updated, Hours logged
- Top achievements
- Upcoming deadlines
- Primary CTA: "View Full Report"

6. TRIAL ENDING EMAIL
Subject: "Your TaskFlow trial ends in 3 days"

Content:
- Reminder about trial ending
- Summary of what they've accomplished
- Benefits of upgrading
- Primary CTA: "Upgrade Now"
- "Have questions? Let's chat"

Design: Simple, clean design. Mobile-responsive. Use web-safe fonts. Limit images for better deliverability.
