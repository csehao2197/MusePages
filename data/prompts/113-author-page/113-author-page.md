Create an author profile page:

1. AUTHOR HEADER
Profile section:
- Large author photo
- Author name (H1)
- Title/role: "Senior Writer" or "Founder"
- Short bio (1-2 paragraphs)
- Location (optional)
- Website link
- Social links (Twitter, LinkedIn, etc.)
- Contact/Email button

Stats:
- Number of posts
- Total views/reads
- Member since

2. FEATURED CONTENT
"Featured Articles" section:
- 3-4 hand-picked or most popular posts
- Larger cards
- Brief description of why featured

3. ALL POSTS
"All Articles by [Author Name]"
- Chronological list (newest first)
- Filter by category (optional)
- Search within author's posts

Post list format:
- Date (grouped by month/year)
- Title
- Category
- Read time
- Brief excerpt on hover (optional)

4. CATEGORIES/TOPICS
"Topics [Author] writes about"
- Category breakdown
- Post count per category
- Visual (pie chart or bars, optional)

5. NEWSLETTER (if author has one)
"Subscribe to [Author's] newsletter"
- Personal pitch
- Sign up form
- Sample of what to expect

6. BOOKS/PRODUCTS (optional)
If author has external work:
- Book covers
- Course thumbnails
- Links to purchase

7. PAGINATION
- Numbered pages
- Or load more

8. SIDEBAR (multi-author blogs)
- Quick author switch
- "Other Authors"
- Author search

Design: Personal but professional. Showcase author's personality. Good reading list UX.
