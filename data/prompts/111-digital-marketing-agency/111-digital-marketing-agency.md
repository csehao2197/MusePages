Create a digital marketing agency website:

1. HERO SECTION
- Results-focused headline
  - "Grow Your Business with Data-Driven Marketing"
  - "We turn clicks into customers"
- Key metrics displayed
  - "$50M+ Revenue Generated"
  - "200+ Clients Served"
  - "35% Average ROI Increase"
- CTA: "Get a Free Audit" or "Book a Strategy Call"

2. SERVICES SECTION
Services offered:
- SEO & Content Marketing
- Paid Advertising (Google, Meta)
- Social Media Marketing
- Email Marketing
- Conversion Rate Optimization
- Analytics & Reporting

Each service:
- Icon
- Title
- Brief description
- Key benefit
- "Learn more" link

3. RESULTS/CASE STUDIES
"Proven Results"
- Client success stories
- Before/after metrics
- Industry and challenge
- Results achieved

Format:
- Stats prominently displayed
- Client testimonial
- "Read Case Study" link

4. PROCESS SECTION
"How We Work"
- Discovery & Audit
- Strategy Development
- Implementation
- Optimization
- Reporting

Show:
- Timeline
- What's included
- Client involvement

5. INDUSTRIES
"Industries We Serve"
- E-commerce
- SaaS
- Healthcare
- Finance
- Real Estate
- Professional Services

6. TOOLS/TECH STACK
"Tools We Use"
- Logo grid of marketing tools
- Google Analytics, SEMrush, HubSpot, etc.
- Certifications (Google Partner, Meta Partner)

7. TEAM SECTION
- Leadership photos
- Roles and expertise
- LinkedIn links
- Team size/culture mention

8. TESTIMONIALS
- Client video testimonials
- Written reviews
- Company logos
- Specific results mentioned

9. BLOG/RESOURCES
- Latest marketing insights
- Free resources (guides, templates)
- Lead magnets

10. CONTACT/CTA
- Free consultation offer
- Contact form
  - Name, email, company
  - Budget range
  - What services interested in
  - Website URL
- Calendar booking embed

Design: Professional, trustworthy. Data and results front and center. Clear CTAs. Build credibility.
