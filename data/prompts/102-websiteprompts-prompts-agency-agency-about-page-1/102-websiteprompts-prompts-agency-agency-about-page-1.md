Create an agency about page:

1. HERO SECTION
- "About Us" or creative headline
  - "The minds behind the magic"
  - "A collective of creative problem solvers"
- Hero image (team photo or office)
- Brief intro statement

2. OUR STORY
- How the agency started
- Founders' vision
- Journey to today
- Milestones achieved
- What drives us

Format:
- Narrative paragraphs
- Or timeline format
- Key moments highlighted

3. MISSION & VALUES
Mission statement:
- What we exist to do
- Who we serve
- Impact we want to make

Values (3-5):
- Creativity
- Collaboration
- Excellence
- Integrity
- Innovation

Each value:
- Value name
- What it means to us
- How we live it

4. TEAM SECTION
Leadership:
- Large photos
- Name and title
- Bio paragraph
- Social links

Team grid:
- All team members
- Photo, name, role
- Fun fact or hobby
- Hover for more info

Consider:
- Pets of the agency
- Office dogs/cats

5. CULTURE
"Life at [Agency]"
- Office photos
- Team events
- Work environment
- Remote/hybrid info

What we offer:
- Benefits highlights
- Growth opportunities
- Work-life balance

6. BY THE NUMBERS
Stats that impress:
- Years in business
- Projects completed
- Team size
- Countries served
- Client retention rate
- Awards won

7. CLIENTS & PARTNERS
- Client logos
- Partner logos
- Testimonials

8. OFFICE/LOCATIONS
- Office photos
- Address(es)
- Map
- Virtual office tour (optional)

9. AWARDS & RECOGNITION
- Award badges
- Press mentions
- Speaking engagements
- Publications

10. CAREERS CTA
- "Join our team"
- Current openings
- Link to careers page

11. CONTACT CTA
- "Get to know us better"
- Schedule a call
- Visit our office

Design: Personal, authentic. Show real people and culture. Build connection.
