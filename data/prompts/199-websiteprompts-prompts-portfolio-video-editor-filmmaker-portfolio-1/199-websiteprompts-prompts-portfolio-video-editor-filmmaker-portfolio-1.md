Create a portfolio for a video editor or filmmaker:

1. HOMEPAGE
- Background video reel (muted, looping)
- Name/logo overlay
- "Watch Reel" button (opens full reel)
- Brief tagline
- Scroll indicator

Alternative homepage:
- Grid of video thumbnails (play on hover)
- Click to watch full video
- Category filter tabs

2. SHOWREEL PAGE
- Embedded video player (Vimeo preferred)
- Full editing reel
- Breakdowns by category:
  - Commercial reel
  - Documentary reel
  - Music video reel
  - Narrative reel

3. WORK/PROJECTS
Categories:
- Commercials
- Music Videos
- Documentaries
- Narrative Films
- Corporate
- Social Content

Each project:
- Thumbnail (video poster)
- Title
- Client/Artist
- Role (Editor, Director, DP)
- Play button overlay
- Click opens video

4. PROJECT PAGE
- Video embed (full-width)
- Project details:
  - Client/Artist
  - Agency (if applicable)
  - Director
  - Your role
  - Year
- Behind the scenes (if available)
- Press/awards
- Related projects

5. ABOUT PAGE
- Professional photo or creative self-portrait
- Bio and background
- Equipment list:
  - Editing: Premiere, Final Cut, DaVinci
  - Motion Graphics: After Effects
  - Color: DaVinci Resolve
- Awards and recognition
- Clients worked with (logos)
- Personal projects and passion

6. SERVICES (if freelance)
- Video editing
- Color grading
- Motion graphics
- Sound design
- Post-production consulting
- Rates or "Contact for quote"

7. CONTACT
- Email
- Phone
- Representation/agent info
- Social: Vimeo, YouTube, Instagram
- Availability status
- Project inquiry form

Design: Dark theme showcases video well. Minimal UI - videos are the focus. Fast loading (lazy load videos). Mobile: Stack layout, videos should still play well.
