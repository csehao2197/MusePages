Create an agency case study page:

1. HERO SECTION
- Client logo or project name
- Project title/tagline
- Hero image (full-width, impactful)
- Quick stats: Results achieved

2. PROJECT OVERVIEW
Quick facts sidebar or header:
- Client name
- Industry
- Services provided
- Project duration
- Team size
- Year

Brief summary:
- What the project was
- Why client came to you
- What you delivered

3. THE CHALLENGE
- Client's situation before
- Problems they faced
- Goals they wanted to achieve
- Constraints or requirements

4. THE APPROACH/STRATEGY
- How you tackled the problem
- Strategic thinking
- Why this approach
- Research conducted

5. THE PROCESS
Step-by-step:
- Discovery phase
- Strategy/planning
- Design phase
- Development phase
- Testing/refinement
- Launch

Include:
- Timeline visual
- Key decisions made
- Collaboration with client

6. THE SOLUTION
Detailed look at deliverables:
- Screenshots/mockups
- Feature highlights
- Design decisions explained
- Technical implementation notes

Visual gallery:
- Multiple project images
- Before/after comparisons
- Different device views
- Detail shots

7. THE RESULTS
Metrics and outcomes:
- Quantifiable results
  - "150% increase in conversions"
  - "50% faster load time"
  - "$2M in new revenue"
- Qualitative outcomes
  - Awards won
  - Press coverage
  - User feedback

8. CLIENT TESTIMONIAL
- Quote from client
- Client name, title, company
- Photo (optional)
- Video testimonial (even better)

9. TEAM CREDITS
- Who worked on this
- Roles (Creative Director, Lead Developer, etc.)
- Link to team page

10. RELATED CASE STUDIES
- "More work like this"
- Similar industry or service
- 2-3 project cards

11. CTA
- "Want results like these?"
- "Start your project"
- Contact link

Design: Visual, storytelling format. Let the work shine. Results prominent.
