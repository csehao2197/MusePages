# The Fox and Lantern: Pub Website Prompt

## 1. Goal
Build a warm, welcoming website for **The Fox and Lantern**, a two hundred year old country pub with rooms in the Cotswolds, known for its Sunday roast and real ales. Deep bottle green background, cream text, brass accents and a characterful serif. It should feel like walking in from the cold to a fire, polished wood and a pint. Photography of food and the bar carries the page.

## 2. Tech Stack
```
React + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion (motion/react) + lucide-react
```
Single page. No component libraries, no booking widget, no map library. Table bookings link out.

## 3. Design system
```css
:root {
  --paper: #13291F;         /* bottle green, main background */
  --ink: #F3EBDD;           /* cream */
  --accent: #C9A15B;        /* brass */
  --on-accent: #13291F;
  --surface: #1B3829;
  --muted: #B9B09F;
  --line: rgba(201,161,91,0.35);
}
```
One cream section in the middle (the menu) uses green text on cream for contrast and rhythm. Every text colour must reach at least 4.5 to 1 against its background. No text under 14px.

## 4. Typography
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Serif+Display:ital@0;1&family=Work+Sans:wght@400;500;600&display=swap" rel="stylesheet" />
```
- **Headings:** DM Serif Display 400, clamp(40px, 5.4vw, 84px), line-height 1.05. Italic for one word per heading.
- **Body:** Work Sans 400, 17px, line-height 1.65
- **Small labels:** Work Sans 600, 14px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.14em, in brass
- **Prices:** Work Sans 500, tabular numbers

## 5. Buttons
Classic and solid:
- **Primary:** brass fill, green text, 6px radius, Work Sans 600 16px, padding 14px 24px. Hover: background lightens slightly and moves up 1px.
- **Secondary:** cream text link with a brass underline 2px, no box.
- No pills, no outline boxes, no arrow circles.

## 6. Sections

1. **Navbar:** green, 80px. Pub name in DM Serif left with a small brass fox outline icon (simple SVG), links centred, `Book a table` primary button right. Turns slightly darker with a brass bottom line after scrolling.
2. **Hero, split:** left half text on green: label `FREE HOUSE SINCE 1824`, the heading, a short paragraph, the primary button and a secondary link. Right half a tall photograph with 12px radius. Under the text a small box with today's opening hours. *Photograph: a cosy pub interior with a lit open fire, dark wooden beams, brass lamps, a pint of amber ale on an oak table in the foreground.*
3. **Three things we do well:** surface coloured. Three columns, each with a photograph on top (4:3, 10px radius), a short heading and two sentences. *Photographs: a Sunday roast with Yorkshire puddings and gravy; four hand pulled ale pumps on a polished bar; a sunny beer garden with wooden tables and hanging lanterns.*
4. **The menu:** cream background, green text. Two columns with menu sections (Starters, Mains, Puddings) and a dotted leader between dish and price. A note about allergens and the Sunday roast under the columns, and a link to the full menu PDF.
5. **On the bar:** green. A list of six cask ales and ciders in two columns, each line with name, brewery, strength and a short tasting note. A brass rule separates the list from the heading.
6. **Rooms upstairs:** a wide photograph on the left, text on the right with three short facts (number of rooms, breakfast, dogs) and a secondary link `See the rooms`. *Photograph: a bright cottage bedroom with exposed beams, a white bed, a wool throw and a window over green fields.*
7. **What's on:** surface. A simple weekly list: day, event, time. Five rows with brass dividers.
8. **Find us:** two columns. Left: address, phone, email and opening hours as a small table. Right: a stylised illustration of the village lane in green and brass lines (SVG), not a real map.
9. **Footer:** dark green. Pub name in DM Serif, a one line welcome, social links and the licensee line.

## 7. Motion
- Hero text fades in from 16px below, 0.6s. Photograph fades and scales from 1.02.
- Menu items appear with a 0.03s stagger when the menu enters view.
- Images inside cards scale to 1.03 on hover over 0.5s.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 8. Responsive
- **Mobile under 768px:** hero stacks, photo first at 4:3. Three things become one column. Menu becomes one column. Ale list one column. Find us stacks. The navbar shows the name and a `Menu` button opening a full screen green panel. A brass `Book a table` bar is fixed at the bottom of the screen with safe area padding.
- **Desktop:** max-width 1200px, section padding 100px.
