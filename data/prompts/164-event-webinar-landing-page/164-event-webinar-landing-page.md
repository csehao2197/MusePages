Create an event/webinar landing page:

1. HERO SECTION
- Event name (large)
- Event type: "Free Webinar" or "Live Workshop"
- Date and time (with timezone)
- Duration
- Host/speaker photos
- "Register Now" CTA

Countdown timer (if applicable):
- Days, hours, minutes, seconds
- Creates urgency

2. EVENT DETAILS
What you'll learn:
- 3-5 key takeaways
- Bullet points with checkmarks
- Specific outcomes

What's covered:
- Agenda or topic outline
- Time breakdown
- Q&A mention

3. SPEAKER/HOST SECTION
For each speaker:
- Professional photo
- Name and title
- Company/brand
- Brief bio
- Credibility points
- Social links

4. WHO IS THIS FOR
"This event is perfect for..."
- Target audience descriptions
- Job titles
- Pain points
- Goals

5. REGISTRATION FORM
Keep it simple:
- Name
- Email
- Company (optional)
- Job title (optional)
- "Register Now" button

Or embedded form (Eventbrite, etc.)

After registration:
- Confirmation message
- "Add to calendar" buttons
- Share buttons

6. SOCIAL PROOF
- "500+ registered"
- Past attendee testimonials
- Company logos attending
- Speaker credentials

7. BONUS/INCENTIVE
"Register now and get..."
- Recording access
- Bonus resource
- Exclusive offer
- Early bird pricing

8. FAQ
- Is this really free?
- Will there be a recording?
- How do I join?
- Can I ask questions?
- What if I can't make it?

9. REMINDER SECTION
What happens after registration:
- Confirmation email
- Reminder emails
- Join link delivery

10. FINAL CTA
- Date/time reminder
- "Save Your Spot"
- Limited spots urgency (if true)

Design: Clean, focused on registration. Show speaker credibility. Countdown creates urgency.
