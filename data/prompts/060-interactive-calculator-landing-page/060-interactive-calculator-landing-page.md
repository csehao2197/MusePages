Create an interactive calculator landing page for lead generation. Include these sections:

1. HERO SECTION
- Headline: "Calculate Your Potential Savings"
- Subheadline: "See exactly how much time and money you could save with [Product Name]"
- Trust indicators: "Used by 10,000+ companies"
- Estimated time: "Takes less than 2 minutes"

2. CALCULATOR INTERFACE
Step-by-step wizard or single-page form:

STEP 1: Company Information
- Company size slider: 1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-500, 500+
- Industry dropdown
- Current solution (if any)

STEP 2: Current Costs
Input fields with helpful tooltips:
- How many hours/week spent on [task]?
- Average hourly rate of team members: $__
- Current tool costs: $__ /month
- Number of team members involved

STEP 3: Goals (optional)
- What's most important to you?
  - [ ] Save time
  - [ ] Reduce costs
  - [ ] Improve accuracy
  - [ ] Scale operations

3. REAL-TIME CALCULATIONS
As user inputs data:
- Show running total/preview
- Animated number updates
- Progress indicator
- "Your estimated savings: $X,XXX/year"

4. RESULTS DISPLAY
After completion, show:

Summary Card (prominent):
- "Your Annual Savings Potential"
- Big number: $47,500/year
- Time saved: 520 hours/year
- ROI: 340%

Breakdown details:
- Current annual cost: $85,000
- Projected cost with us: $37,500
- Net savings: $47,500
- Payback period: 3.2 months

Visualization:
- Before/after comparison bars
- Pie chart of cost breakdown
- Timeline to ROI

5. PERSONALIZED RECOMMENDATIONS
Based on inputs:
- "Based on your company size, we recommend the [Plan Name] plan"
- Top 3 features that would benefit them
- Similar company success story

6. LEAD CAPTURE (Results Gate)
To get full report:
- "Get Your Detailed Report"
- Work email (required)
- Name
- Phone (optional)
- Company name
- "Send My Report" button

Or show partial results with "unlock full analysis" CTA

7. SOCIAL PROOF ALONGSIDE RESULTS
- "Companies like yours save an average of $X"
- Customer logos
- Relevant testimonial
- "Join 500+ companies already saving"

8. COMPARISON SECTION
"How you compare:"
- Your industry average
- Top performers in your sector
- Where you could be

9. DETAILED BREAKDOWN (Expandable)
Line-item savings:
- Labor cost savings: $32,000
- Tool consolidation: $8,000
- Error reduction: $5,000
- Time savings value: $2,500

10. NEXT STEPS CTA
Multiple options:
- "Start Free Trial" (primary)
- "Schedule a Demo" (secondary)
- "Download PDF Report"
- "Share Results" (email/social)

11. METHODOLOGY NOTE
Transparency section:
- "How we calculated this"
- Assumptions explained
- Links to case studies
- "Your results may vary" disclaimer

12. FAQ SECTION
- "How accurate is this calculator?"
- "What data do you use?"
- "Is my information secure?"
- "How do I get started?"

13. ALTERNATIVE CTA
For users not ready:
- "Not ready yet? Get our ROI Guide"
- Email capture for nurture sequence
- Bookmark/save results option

14. EXIT INTENT
If user attempts to leave without converting:
- "Wait! Don't lose your results"
- Offer to email results
- Lighter commitment CTA

Design: Clean, focused interface. Minimal distractions. Large input fields. Progress bar if multi-step. Mobile-friendly sliders and inputs. Trust signals throughout. Results feel personalized and valuable.
