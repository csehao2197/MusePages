Create a lead magnet landing page:

1. HERO SECTION
- Compelling headline
  - "Free [Resource]: [Benefit]"
  - "Download Your Free [Resource]"
- Resource preview (ebook cover, checklist preview)
- Brief description of what they get
- Email capture form
- "Get Free Access" button

2. WHAT'S INSIDE
Preview the value:
- Chapter list (if ebook)
- Checklist items (if checklist)
- Template preview (if template)
- 5-7 key points covered

3. WHO IS THIS FOR
"This [resource] is for you if..."
- Target audience pain points
- Goals they have
- Where they are in journey

4. BENEFITS
What they'll achieve:
- Specific outcomes
- Time/money saved
- Problems solved
- Skills gained

5. AUTHOR CREDIBILITY
- Who created this
- Photo and bio
- Credentials
- Why they're qualified

6. SOCIAL PROOF
- Download count
- Testimonials from downloaders
- Companies who've used it
- Star ratings

7. SAMPLE CONTENT
Give a taste:
- One page preview
- One tip from the guide
- Mini excerpt
- Screenshot of template

8. WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
After download:
- Instant access
- Email delivery
- No spam promise

9. FAQ
- Is it really free?
- What format is it in?
- Will I get spammed?
- Can I share it?

10. EMAIL FORM
Simple fields:
- Name (optional)
- Email address
- "Download Now" button

Add trust:
- "We respect your privacy"
- "Unsubscribe anytime"
- Privacy policy link

11. FINAL CTA
- Repeat the offer
- Urgency if applicable
- One more form

Design: Single goal - get the email. Remove all distractions. Make the resource look valuable.
