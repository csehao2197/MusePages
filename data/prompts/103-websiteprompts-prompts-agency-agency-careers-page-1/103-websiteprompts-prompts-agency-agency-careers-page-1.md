Create an agency careers page:

1. HERO SECTION
- "Join Our Team" or creative headline
- "Help us create amazing work for amazing clients"
- Team photo or culture video
- CTA: "View Open Positions"

2. WHY JOIN US
"Why work at [Agency]"
- 4-6 benefits/perks

Examples:
- Creative freedom
- Meaningful work
- Great clients
- Growth opportunities
- Work-life balance
- Amazing team

Each with icon, title, description

3. CULTURE SECTION
"Life at [Agency]"
- Photos of office/team
- Day in the life
- Team activities
- Remote work policy

Values lived:
- How values show up daily
- Team testimonials

4. BENEFITS & PERKS
Comprehensive list:
- Health insurance
- Retirement/401k
- Unlimited PTO
- Remote flexibility
- Professional development budget
- Conference attendance
- Equipment allowance
- Team retreats
- Wellness programs
- Parental leave

5. OPEN POSITIONS
Job listings:
- Position title
- Department
- Location (Remote/Office)
- Type (Full-time/Contract)
- Brief description
- "Apply Now" button

Filters:
- By department
- By location
- By type

No positions:
- "No current openings"
- "Send us your resume anyway"
- General application form

6. JOB DETAIL PAGE
For each position:
- Title and details
- About the role
- Responsibilities (bullet list)
- Requirements
  - Must have
  - Nice to have
- Benefits
- Salary range (if sharing)
- Application form or link

7. APPLICATION PROCESS
What to expect:
- Application review
- Phone screen
- Interview rounds
- Culture fit
- Offer

Timeline expectations

8. TEAM TESTIMONIALS
"What our team says"
- Employee quotes
- Video testimonials
- Day in the life features

9. DIVERSITY & INCLUSION
- Commitment statement
- Initiatives
- Employee resource groups
- Stats (if appropriate)

10. GENERAL APPLICATION
For those who don't see a fit:
- "Don't see your role?"
- General application form
- Resume upload
- "Tell us how you can contribute"

11. CONTACT HR
- Questions about careers
- HR email
- LinkedIn page

Design: Energetic, authentic. Show real culture. Make applying easy.
