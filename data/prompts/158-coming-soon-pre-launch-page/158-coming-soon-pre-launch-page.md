Create a coming soon / pre-launch landing page:

1. HERO SECTION
- Product/Company name
- "Coming Soon" badge
- Compelling tagline
- Launch date (if known)
- Countdown timer
- Email signup: "Get notified at launch"

2. TEASER CONTENT
What's coming:
- Brief product description
- Problem it solves
- Key benefit
- Mystery/intrigue element

Visual teaser:
- Blurred screenshots
- Partial product reveal
- Concept illustrations
- Silhouette reveal

3. EMAIL SIGNUP FORM
- Email input
- "Join the Waitlist" button
- Subscriber count: "5,000+ waiting"

After signup:
- Thank you message
- Position in line (optional)
- Share to move up (optional)
- Social share buttons

4. SNEAK PEEK
"Here's what to expect"
- 3-4 feature teasers
- Icons and brief descriptions
- "And much more..."

5. PROGRESS/TIMELINE
Show development progress:
- Progress bar
- Milestone checklist
- "Currently in beta"
- "Launching Q1 2025"

6. SOCIAL PROOF
- Beta tester quotes
- Industry expert endorsement
- Company backing
- Press mentions

7. EARLY ACCESS OFFER
- "Early supporters get..."
- Founding member pricing
- Exclusive features
- Early access

8. ABOUT/STORY
Brief about section:
- Who's building this
- Why it's being built
- Vision for the future

9. FAQ
- When is launch?
- How much will it cost?
- How is my data used?
- Can I get early access?

10. SOCIAL LINKS
- Follow for updates
- Twitter, LinkedIn, etc.
- Blog/content updates

11. FOOTER
- Contact email
- Privacy policy
- Press inquiries

Design: Build anticipation. Create FOMO. Make signup irresistible. Can be single-page or minimal.
