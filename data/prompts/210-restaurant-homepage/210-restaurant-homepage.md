1. HERO SECTION
- Full-width background image of signature dish or restaurant interior
- Restaurant name in elegant serif typography

2. INTRODUCTION SECTION

3. FEATURED DISHES (3-column grid)
- 3 signature dishes with beautiful food photography
- Dish name, brief description, and price
- "View Full Menu" button below

4. AMBIANCE GALLERY
- 4-6 images showing restaurant interior, bar area, private dining
- Masonry or grid layout
- Subtle hover effects

5. RESERVATIONS SECTION
- Headline: "Reserve Your Table"
- Embedded reservation form or OpenTable widget placeholder
- Operating hours displayed
- Phone number for direct reservations

6. TESTIMONIALS
- 3 customer reviews with ratings
- Names and "Verified Diner" badges

7. LOCATION & CONTACT
- Embedded Google Maps
- Full address
- Phone, email
- Parking information

8. FOOTER
- Social media links (Instagram, Facebook, Yelp)
- Newsletter signup
- Links to Menu, About, Private Events, Careers
