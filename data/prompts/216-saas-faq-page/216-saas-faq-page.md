Create a comprehensive FAQ page for a SaaS product. Include these sections:

1. HERO SECTION
- Headline: "Frequently Asked Questions"
- Subheadline: "Find answers to common questions about our product"
- Search bar (prominent, centered)
- Placeholder: "Search for answers..."
- Popular searches as quick links below

2. CATEGORY NAVIGATION
Horizontal tabs or sidebar with categories:
- Getting Started
- Billing & Pricing
- Account & Security
- Features & Usage
- Integrations
- Technical Support
- Enterprise & Teams

3. FAQ ACCORDION SECTIONS

GETTING STARTED (6-8 questions):
- "How do I create an account?"
- "What's included in the free trial?"
- "How do I invite team members?"
- "Where can I find tutorials?"
- "Is there a mobile app?"
- "How do I import my data?"

BILLING & PRICING (6-8 questions):
- "What payment methods do you accept?"
- "Can I change my plan anytime?"
- "Do you offer refunds?"
- "How does annual billing work?"
- "What happens when my trial ends?"
- "Are there any hidden fees?"
- "Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?"

ACCOUNT & SECURITY (5-6 questions):
- "How do I reset my password?"
- "How do I enable two-factor authentication?"
- "Can I export my data?"
- "How do I delete my account?"
- "Is my data encrypted?"
- "Where is my data stored?"

FEATURES & USAGE (6-8 questions):
- "What are the usage limits?"
- "How do I use [key feature]?"
- "Can I customize the dashboard?"
- "How do notifications work?"
- "Can I automate tasks?"

4. ACCORDION BEHAVIOR
- Click to expand/collapse
- Smooth animation
- Plus/minus or chevron icon
- Only one open at a time (optional)
- Deep linking to specific questions

5. SEARCH FUNCTIONALITY
- Real-time filtering as user types
- Highlight matching text
- "No results found" state with suggestions
- Category filter alongside search

6. HELPFUL FEATURES
- "Was this helpful?" Yes/No buttons on each answer
- Copy link to answer
- Share on social
- Print-friendly version

7. STILL NEED HELP? SECTION
- "Can't find what you're looking for?"
- Contact support button (opens chat or email)
- Link to documentation
- Community forum link
- Schedule a call option

8. RELATED RESOURCES
- Link to full documentation
- Video tutorials
- Blog posts
- Webinar recordings
- API reference

9. FOOTER CTA
- "Ready to get started?"
- Sign up or contact sales buttons

Design: Clean, scannable layout. Clear category separation. Mobile-friendly accordions. Search prominent. Fast load with all content visible to search engines.
