Create a creative agency homepage:

1. HERO SECTION
- Bold, attention-grabbing headline
  - "We create brands that matter"
  - "Digital experiences that move people"
- Subheadline with value proposition
- Primary CTA: "Start a Project" or "Let's Talk"
- Secondary CTA: "View Our Work"

Visual options:
- Video background (showreel)
- Animated typography
- Interactive 3D element
- Bold color blocks
- Featured project preview

2. SHOWREEL/WORK PREVIEW
- Embedded video or
- Auto-playing project carousel
- "Watch Reel" button

3. SERVICES SECTION
"What We Do"
- Brand Strategy
- Visual Identity
- Web Design & Development
- Digital Marketing
- Content Creation
- Motion & Video

Format:
- Grid with icons and descriptions
- Or interactive accordion
- "Learn more" links to service pages

4. FEATURED WORK
- 3-4 hero projects
- Large images/videos
- Client name
- Project type
- Brief description
- Click to view case study

Layout:
- Asymmetric grid
- Full-width alternating
- Masonry style

5. CLIENTS SECTION
- "Trusted by" or "Brands we've worked with"
- Logo grid (grayscale, color on hover)
- Mix of recognizable and aspirational

6. ABOUT SNIPPET
- Brief agency intro
- Key differentiator
- Team photo or office shot
- "About Us" link

7. AWARDS/RECOGNITION
- Award badges
- "Awwwards Site of the Day"
- Industry recognition

8. TESTIMONIALS
- Client testimonials
- Video testimonials (even better)
- Results/metrics

9. PROCESS TEASER
"How We Work"
- 4 step overview
- Links to detailed process page

10. CTA SECTION
- "Let's create something amazing"
- Contact form or "Get in Touch" button
- Office locations

11. FOOTER
- Navigation
- Social links
- Newsletter signup
- Contact info
- Careers link

Design: Bold, confident, creative. Show personality. Push boundaries while staying usable.
