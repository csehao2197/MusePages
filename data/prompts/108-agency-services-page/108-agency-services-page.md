Create an agency services page:

1. PAGE HEADER
- "Our Services" or specific service name
- Brief intro explaining what you offer
- "Get a Quote" CTA

2. SERVICE OVERVIEW
List all services with:
- Icon
- Service name
- One-line description
- Link to detailed section or page

3. INDIVIDUAL SERVICE SECTIONS
For each service:

Header:
- Service name (H2)
- Service description (2-3 paragraphs)
- Key benefits (bullet list)

What's Included:
- Detailed deliverables list
- What client gets
- Timeline expectations

Process:
- Steps specific to this service
- What the agency does
- What the client provides

Pricing (optional):
- Starting from $X
- Or "Custom quote"
- What affects pricing

Examples:
- Mini case studies
- Before/after
- Results

CTA:
- "Inquire about [Service]"
- Service-specific form

4. SERVICE PACKAGES (if applicable)
Tiered offerings:
- Basic / Standard / Premium
- What's included in each
- Price or "Starting from"
- Best for [type of client]

5. TOOLS & METHODS
- Software/tools used
- Methodologies (Agile, etc.)
- Certifications

6. FAQ FOR SERVICES
- How long does this take?
- What do you need from us?
- How do you handle revisions?
- Do you offer ongoing support?

7. TESTIMONIALS
Service-specific testimonials:
- Clients who used this service
- Results they achieved
- Recommend relevant quotes

8. RELATED SERVICES
"Services that pair well"
- Cross-sell complementary services
- Package deals

9. CTA SECTION
- "Ready to get started?"
- Contact options
- Free consultation offer

Design: Organized, scannable. Easy to find specific service info. Clear CTAs.
