Create a portfolio website for a writer/author:

1. HOMEPAGE
- Clean, typography-focused design
- Author name prominently displayed
- Tagline: "Fiction Writer | Essayist | Storyteller"
- Featured quote from their work
- Latest book/publication highlight
- Newsletter signup
- "Read My Work" CTA

2. BOOKS/PUBLICATIONS PAGE
For each book:
- Cover image
- Title and subtitle
- Publication date
- Genre tags
- Brief synopsis (back cover copy)
- Buy links (Amazon, indie bookstores)
- Reviews/praise quotes
- Awards badges

Layout: Grid or list view toggle

3. BOOK DETAIL PAGE
- Large cover image
- Full synopsis
- Excerpt (first chapter)
- Reviews section
- Press mentions
- Buy links (multiple retailers)
- Reading guide (for book clubs)
- Related books

4. ARTICLES/ESSAYS PAGE
- Published articles list
- Filterable by publication or topic
- Each entry: Title, publication, date, excerpt
- Link to original or full text

5. ABOUT/BIO PAGE
- Author photo (professional)
- Full bio (different lengths: short, medium, long)
- Writing journey story
- Influences and inspirations
- FAQ for readers
- Downloadable press kit

6. SPEAKING/EVENTS
- Upcoming events calendar
- Past speaking engagements
- Topics they speak on
- Booking inquiry form
- Speaking fees (or "Contact for rates")

7. FOR MEDIA/PRESS
- High-res photos
- Bio in various lengths
- Book covers and assets
- Interview availability
- Contact for press inquiries

8. NEWSLETTER/BLOG
- Signup form (prominent)
- Archive of past newsletters
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Writing tips
- Personal essays

9. CONTACT
- Literary agent info
- Email for general inquiries
- Form for speaking requests
- Social media links

Design: Elegant, bookish feel. Serif fonts work well. Warm, inviting colors. Consider a texture that evokes paper or books.
