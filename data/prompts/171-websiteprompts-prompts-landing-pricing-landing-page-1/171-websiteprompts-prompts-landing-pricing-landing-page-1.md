Create a pricing landing page:

1. HERO SECTION
- "Simple, Transparent Pricing"
- "Choose the plan that fits your needs"
- Billing toggle: Monthly / Annual (show savings)

2. PRICING CARDS (3 tiers recommended)

TIER 1 - Starter/Free:
- Tier name
- Price: $0 or $X/month
- "Best for individuals" tagline
- Feature list (5-8 items)
- Limitations noted
- CTA: "Get Started" (outline button)

TIER 2 - Pro/Growth (highlighted):
- "Most Popular" badge
- Tier name
- Price with annual savings
- "Best for teams" tagline
- Everything in Starter +
- Additional features
- CTA: "Start Free Trial" (solid button)

TIER 3 - Enterprise:
- Tier name
- "Custom pricing" or starting price
- "Best for large organizations"
- Everything in Pro +
- Enterprise features (SSO, SLA, etc.)
- CTA: "Contact Sales"

3. FEATURE COMPARISON TABLE
Full feature matrix:
- All features listed
- Checkmarks/X for each tier
- Grouped by category
- Expandable on mobile

4. FAQ SECTION
Pricing questions:
- Can I change plans later?
- What payment methods accepted?
- Is there a free trial?
- What's your refund policy?
- Do you offer discounts?
- What counts as a user/seat?

5. MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE
- "30-day money-back guarantee"
- "No questions asked"
- Risk reversal messaging

6. TESTIMONIALS
- Customers on each tier
- ROI testimonials
- "Worth every penny" quotes

7. ENTERPRISE SECTION
For larger customers:
- Custom solutions
- Volume discounts
- Dedicated support
- Security compliance
- "Talk to Sales" CTA

8. CALCULATOR (optional)
- Cost calculator
- Based on usage
- "Your estimated cost: $X/month"

9. TRUST SIGNALS
- Security badges
- Customer logos
- Uptime guarantee
- Data protection

10. FINAL CTA
- "Start your free trial today"
- "Questions? Contact us"

Design: Clear comparison. Highlight recommended plan. Remove friction. Answer objections.
