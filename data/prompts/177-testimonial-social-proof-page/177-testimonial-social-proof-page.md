Create a testimonials/social proof landing page:

1. HERO SECTION
- "Loved by thousands of customers"
- Overall rating: "4.9/5 from 2,000+ reviews"
- Featured testimonial (large)
- Review source logos (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot)

2. STATS BAR
Quick proof:
- "10,000+ customers"
- "4.9★ average rating"
- "95% satisfaction rate"
- "$50M+ value delivered"

3. VIDEO TESTIMONIALS
- 3-4 video testimonials
- Customer name and company
- Brief quote as preview
- Play button

4. TESTIMONIAL GRID
Multiple testimonials:
- Customer photo
- Full name and title
- Company and logo
- Star rating
- Quote text
- Date

Filters:
- By industry
- By company size
- By use case

5. CASE STUDY PREVIEWS
"Customer Success Stories"
- Company logo
- Challenge summary
- Results achieved
- "Read full story" link

6. REVIEW AGGREGATION
- G2 rating widget
- Capterra rating widget
- Trustpilot widget
- App store ratings

7. LOGOS SECTION
"Trusted by leading companies"
- Logo grid
- Mix of sizes
- Industry variety

8. QUOTES BY CATEGORY
Group by use case:
- "What customers say about [Feature 1]"
- "What customers say about [Feature 2]"
- "What customers say about Support"

9. USER-GENERATED CONTENT
- Social media mentions
- Tweet embeds
- LinkedIn posts
- Reddit mentions

10. SUBMIT TESTIMONIAL
"Share your experience"
- Simple form
- Photo upload
- Rating
- Written review

11. CTA SECTION
- "Join our happy customers"
- "Start your free trial"
- "See how we can help you"

Design: Let customers sell for you. Mix of formats (text, video, stats). Make it scannable.
