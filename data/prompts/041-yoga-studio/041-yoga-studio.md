Design: Warm, serene, organic. Primary: #7C6F5B (warm taupe), secondary: #2DD4BF (soft teal for accents and CTAs), accent: #F59E0B (amber for badges and highlights). Backgrounds: #FFFFFF (white) alternating with #FDF8F0 (warm cream) and one dark #292524 (warm charcoal) section for contrast. Typography: Cormorant Garamond for headings (elegant serif), Inter for body text. Cards with 16px border-radius, minimal shadows, organic rounded shapes. Plenty of whitespace, natural textures (linen, warm wood tones in photos). Muted, earth-tone color palette throughout.

Responsive: On mobile, class type cards become horizontal swipe. Schedule collapses to accordion by time slot. Instructor cards become 2-column then single column. Pricing cards stack with "Most Popular" first. On-demand thumbnails become 2x2 grid. Location cards stack vertically. Floating "Book Class" button at bottom on mobile. Navigation collapses to hamburger with cream background overlay.
