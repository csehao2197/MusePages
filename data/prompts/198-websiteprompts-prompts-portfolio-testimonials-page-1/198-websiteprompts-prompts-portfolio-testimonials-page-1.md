Create a dedicated testimonials page for a freelancer or agency portfolio. Include these sections:

1. HERO SECTION
- Headline: "What Our Clients Say"
- Subheadline: "Real stories from real people we've had the pleasure of working with"
- Overall stats bar:
  - 150+ Happy Clients
  - 4.9/5 Average Rating
  - 98% Would Recommend
  - 50+ 5-Star Reviews

2. FEATURED TESTIMONIAL
Large, prominent showcase:
- Full video testimonial (or large photo)
- Extended quote (2-3 paragraphs)
- Client name, title, company
- Company logo
- Project type or service used
- "View Case Study" link

3. FILTER/SORT OPTIONS
- Filter by industry: Tech, E-commerce, Healthcare, etc.
- Filter by service: Web Design, Branding, Development
- Sort by: Most Recent, Highest Rated, Featured

4. TESTIMONIAL GRID
Card layout (3 columns desktop, responsive):

Each card includes:
- Client photo (or company logo)
- Star rating (1-5)
- Quote excerpt (150-200 chars)
- "Read more" expansion
- Client name
- Job title
- Company name
- Industry tag
- Date
- Verified badge if applicable

5. VIDEO TESTIMONIALS SECTION
- Dedicated row for video testimonials
- Thumbnail grid with play button overlay
- Client name and company below
- Lightbox video player
- 3-4 featured videos

6. LOGO WALL
"Trusted by companies like:"
- Grid of client logos
- Grayscale, color on hover
- Link to related case study
- 15-20 recognizable logos

7. SUCCESS METRICS
Visual showcase:
- "Our clients see results"
- Big numbers with context:
  - "250% average increase in conversions"
  - "$10M+ revenue generated for clients"
  - "45% average cost reduction"
  - "2x faster time to market"

8. LONG-FORM SUCCESS STORY
One detailed story with:
- The challenge (before state)
- The solution (what we did)
- The results (after state)
- Client quote throughout
- Photos/screenshots
- "Read Full Case Study" CTA

9. REVIEW PLATFORM BADGES
- Google Reviews (4.9 stars)
- Clutch rating
- G2 badge
- Trustpilot score
- "Read all reviews on [platform]" links

10. SUBMIT A REVIEW CTA
- "Had a great experience? Share your story"
- Button to review form or third-party platform
- Brief form for written testimonials

11. FINAL CTA SECTION
- "Ready to become our next success story?"
- "Start Your Project" button
- "Schedule a Consultation" link
- Contact information

Design: Clean, trustworthy. Let the testimonials shine. High-quality photos. Subtle animations on scroll. White/light background for professionalism. Generous whitespace.
