Beyond the postcard

A thousand little worlds.
One extraordinary city.

Start with a quiet shrine, follow the scent of coffee down a side street, and end beneath a sky of neon. Tokyo rewards the unhurried traveler. This sample itinerary is an invitation to explore, not a live travel service.

A little structure. Plenty of possibility.

Three days to find your rhythm.

DAY 01 / OLD TOKYO

Start with a little stillness.

Explore Asakusa, take in the grounds of Senso-ji, and wander toward the Sumida River for an afternoon walk.

DAY 02 / CITY ENERGY

Follow the bright lights.

Find a quiet moment around Meiji Shrine before exploring Harajuku's side streets and Shibuya after sunset.

DAY 03 / YOUR OWN WAY

Leave room for discovery.

Browse a neighborhood bookshop, linger over a coffee, or spend an afternoon exploring a museum of your choice.

Before you wander

Small tips, better days.

Travel thoughtfully.
Respect local signage and quiet spaces. Check current opening hours and entry requirements before visiting.

Keep plans flexible.
Leave time between neighborhoods. The most memorable moments rarely follow an exact schedule.
