DESIGN SYSTEM
- Primary: #D97706 (amber-600) for CTAs, accents, and interactive elements
- Secondary: #1C1917 (stone-900) for dark backgrounds and headers
- Body text: #44403C (stone-700) on light backgrounds, #E7E5E4 (stone-200) on dark
- Accent: #FEF3C7 (amber-100) for badges and highlights
- Section backgrounds alternate: white, #FAFAF9 (stone-50), #292524 (stone-800)
- Typography: Oswald or Barlow Condensed for headings (uppercase, tight tracking), Inter for body text
- Textural elements: subtle grain/noise overlay on dark sections, copper/amber gradient accents on borders
- Cards: 12px border-radius, warm-toned shadows (0 2px 12px rgba(28,25,23,0.08))
- Photography: warm, amber-toned, atmospheric lighting — no sterile product shots
