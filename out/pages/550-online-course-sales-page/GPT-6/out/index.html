A HUMAN ON THE OTHER SIDE

Hi, I’m Theo.
I used to make
unfinished games.

My folders had impressive names. “Adventure_final.” “Adventure_final_v2.” The games themselves? Not so final.

Things changed when I made something small enough to finish. A single room. A character. A reason to press play again. That’s the feeling this workshop is built around.

I’ll take you from your first line of logic to a game you can send to a friend. Slowly, clearly, with the occasional extremely reasonable duck.
