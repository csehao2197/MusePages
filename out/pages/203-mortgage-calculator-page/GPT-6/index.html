How is my payment calculated?

Monthly P&I uses L × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)−n), where L is the loan, r is annual interest/12 and n is monthly payments. At 0% interest, P&I is loan/months. Annual tax and insurance are divided by 12; HOA and estimated PMI are added.

What is PMI and how can I avoid it?

Private mortgage insurance may apply on some loans below 20% down. This tool assumes 0.5% annually of the original loan, held constant in the monthly estimate. Eligibility, price and cancellation depend on lender and loan program.

Fixed vs adjustable rate

A fixed rate stays the same during the modeled term. An ARM may change after an initial period; this tool does not forecast adjustments. The ARM figure is illustrative only.

How much house can I afford?

Consider income, existing debts, savings, closing costs, maintenance and your own comfort level. A payment estimate alone does not determine eligibility or an affordable budget.
