Common questions.

How long does it take to learn?

Everyone learns differently. Your instructor should assess readiness with you; a five-day course cannot guarantee a pass.

What car is used?

The example fleet is a modern dual-control Ford Focus or similar. Confirm the vehicle and insurance with the provider.

Can I choose my instructor?

You can request James or Priya in this local demo. Availability is not connected.

What if I fail my test?

A debrief and targeted follow-up lessons can help you plan your next steps.
