A THOUGHTFUL LITTLE CONCEPT

A workspace, not a connected life.

OmniLife OS is a self-contained, fictional browser demo. All changes live in this tab’s memory. Refreshing restores the samples.

  • Learning: unit progress, assignment statuses, exact-second stopwatch logs, and per-path daily journals.
  • Finances: sample USD balances, bills, explicit next occurrences, partial receipts and a linked session ledger. No real money moves.
  • Commands: fixed grammar and atomic local actions, not autonomous AI. No OpenAI, Claude, Supabase, Prisma, login or server actions.
  • Projects: a small session kanban. Calendar: a UTC daily agenda of assignments, unpaid bills and two sample time blocks. Hobby content is inspiration, not a tracking system.
