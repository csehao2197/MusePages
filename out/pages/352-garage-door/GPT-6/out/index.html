Safety / A word about springs

A garage door spring holds enough tension to lift seventy kilos. A damaged spring can release that force suddenly. Spring replacement is work for a trained professional with the right equipment. If a spring breaks, stop using the door, keep the opening clear and call for advice. There is no home repair procedure on this page.
