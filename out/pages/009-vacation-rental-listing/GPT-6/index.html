About this property

Welcome to Casa Azul — your private slice of the Tulum coastline. This handcrafted 3-bedroom villa sits directly on the beach, surrounded by lush jungle gardens and the sound of Caribbean waves.

Built in 2022 with sustainable local materials — reclaimed tropical hardwood, Yucatán limestone and handmade Oaxacan tiles — every detail was designed for relaxed luxury. Wake up to ocean views, swim in the infinity pool at sunset, and fall asleep to the rhythm of the sea.

The space

2,400 sq ft across two levels. Downstairs: open-air living and dining, a chef's kitchen and two en-suite guest bedrooms with direct pool access. Upstairs: the master suite, a private balcony, an outdoor rain shower and a 180° ocean panorama.

Guest access

The whole property is yours: a private beach path, rooftop terrace with daybed, outdoor BBQ, yoga deck and beach palapa. Beach towels, snorkel gear and two bicycles are included.
