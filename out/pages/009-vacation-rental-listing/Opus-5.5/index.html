About This Property

Welcome to Casa Azul — your private slice of the Tulum coastline. This handcrafted 3-bedroom villa sits directly on the beach, surrounded by lush jungle gardens and the sound of Caribbean waves.

Built in 2022 with sustainable local materials (reclaimed tropical hardwood, Yucatán limestone, handmade Oaxacan tiles), every detail was designed for relaxed luxury. Wake up to ocean views from every bedroom, swim in the infinity pool at sunset, and fall asleep to the rhythm of the sea.

The space 2,400 sq ft open-plan villa across two levels. Ground floor: open-air living and dining area, fully equipped chef's kitchen, two guest bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms, direct pool access. Upper level: master suite with private balcony, outdoor rain shower, and 180° ocean panorama.

Guest access Full property including private beach path, rooftop terrace with daybed, outdoor BBQ area, yoga deck, and a palapa (thatched-roof shade structure) on the beach. Complimentary beach towels, snorkel gear, and 2 bicycles.
