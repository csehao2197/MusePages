    COMMON QUESTIONS

    A little less unknown.

    How long does a mortgage take?

    Times vary with lender checks, property valuation and legal work. No completion or comparison timeframe is guaranteed here.

    Do I need a large deposit?

    Deposit requirements depend on the lender, product and circumstances. The calculator shows loan to value, not eligibility or approval.

    Can I apply if I’m self-employed?

    Criteria vary. Explore the self-employed checklist for general preparation examples and speak to an authorised adviser.

    What documents might I need?

    Identity, address, income, bank statements and deposit-source evidence are common examples. This page accepts no uploads or sensitive financial records.
