03 / THE PROCESSFROM THE FIRST QUESTION TO THE LAST PIXEL

STAGES OF WEBSITE
DEVELOPMENT

Great work starts with a good conversation. We define your goals, audience, scope, and success criteria — and make sure we’re solving the right problem before drawing the first line.

Audience research, a competitive landscape, and a clear content map turn assumptions into informed decisions. The deliverable is a focused experience strategy, not a pile of charts.

We explore journeys and test the important moments with interactive wireframes. Early feedback lets us refine navigation and functionality before committing to visual details.

Typography, color, composition, and motion become one cohesive visual language. I craft responsive screens and a reusable design system, with accessibility built into the decisions.

The experience is refined for small screens, touch, keyboard navigation, and reduced motion. Every breakpoint gets considered attention — not just a smaller desktop layout.

We check the details, validate the key journeys, and prepare a clear handoff. You receive the final designs, documented components, and a thoughtful foundation for the next chapter.
