A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your first hello.

Do I need any experience?

No. A1 assumes no prior study, and the quiz helps place everyone else.

How accurate is the placement quiz?

It recommends a starting level from ten answers; a teacher can adjust it after a short conversation. In this English-only adaptation, questions assess learning concepts rather than direct language proficiency.

Can I switch levels?

Yes. Speak with your teacher after the first class and the school will suggest a better-fit group.

What should I bring?

A notebook and something to write with; all course materials are included.

Are online classes live?

Yes. Online groups meet with a teacher at the listed recurring time and are not prerecorded.

What is the refund policy?

A full refund is available up to seven days before the first class; after that, the school offers course credit. This is an illustrative policy for the fictional school, not a real offer.
