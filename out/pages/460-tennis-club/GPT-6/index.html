OFF COURT IS HALF THE POINT

Stay for
one more.

Four artificial clay courts, resurfaced in 2024. Two acrylic hard courts; court six has an airhall November–March. Member-card floodlights until 10pm.

Showers, changing rooms, a viewing balcony, and the clubhouse bar Thursday–Sunday. Tuesday and Thursday matches start at 6:30pm. There is always tea afterwards.

Men: Yorkshire Divisions 2, 4, 6. Ladies: Divisions 1, 3, 5. Mixed: Division 2.
