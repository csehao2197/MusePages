      About KVOLT

      Born Kevin Volt in East Atlanta, I started making beats on a cracked copy of FL Studio at 14. Twelve years later, I've produced for Grammy-nominated artists, scored commercial campaigns, and built a catalog of over 500 original beats.

      My sound sits at the intersection of trap, R&B, and cinematic production — heavy 808s with melodic layers that give artists space to tell their stories. I've worked out of Tree Sound Studios, Patchwerk Recording, and my private studio in Midtown Atlanta.

      Whether you need a single beat for your next release or a full album's worth of production, I bring the same energy and attention to detail to every session.
