Born Kevin Volt in East Atlanta, I started making beats in FL Studio at 14. Twelve years later, I've produced for Grammy-nominated artists, scored commercial campaigns, and built a catalog of over 500 original beats.

My sound sits at the intersection of trap, R&B, and cinematic production — heavy 808s with melodic layers that give artists space to tell their stories. I've worked out of Tree Sound Studios, Patchwerk Recording, and my private studio in Midtown Atlanta.
