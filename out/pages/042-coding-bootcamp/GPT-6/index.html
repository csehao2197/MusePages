BIG QUESTIONS ARE WELCOME

You’re probably
wondering…

Choosing a new path is a big decision.
Let’s start with a little clarity.

Do I need prior coding experience?

No. These example programs are designed for beginners, with a prep module to introduce the fundamentals. A real program would assess readiness and explain admissions criteria.

What is the class schedule?

Example full-time: Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM. Part-time: Tuesday/Thursday evenings plus Saturdays. Exact time zone, holidays and availability would be confirmed by an actual provider.

What happens if I don’t get a job?

There is no employment guarantee. The illustrative ISA uses a $50K earnings threshold; actual payment share, cap, term, deferment and eligibility would require a written agreement. No ISA is offered here.

Can I visit the campus?

The concept includes Saturday tours, but this fictional academy has no verified campus. Use an event preview to explore the interface; no real visit is booked.

How much time should I set aside?

Full-time development and data tracks run 12 weeks; UX runs 10 weeks. Part-time tracks are 24 and 20 weeks respectively. Allow additional time for projects and preparation.

Is the academy licensed or accredited?

Licensing and accreditation are not verified. The badges are design examples, not evidence of state approval, transfer credit, certification or eligibility for public funding.

Can I download a syllabus?

Yes. Each program footer includes a local sample syllabus outlining its topics and capstone. These are illustrative curricula, not binding course catalogs.

How are outcomes calculated?

The displayed figures are fictional. The example report explains denominators, reporting windows, response rates and exclusions a real audited report would need; it does not claim that such an audit occurred.
