COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things to know.

Do I need an NHS GP?

Private care can complement NHS care. Discuss registration and consent for sharing records with a real provider.

Can I use insurance?

Check your own policy and provider. This demo cannot verify cover, issue receipts or bill an insurer.

Can you prescribe?

This website cannot prescribe. A qualified clinician must assess suitability; prescription and dispensing charges may apply.

Can I see the same doctor?

Choose an example preference in the planner. A real practice must confirm continuity and availability.

Do you see children?

The brief includes children’s appointments; this demo provides no paediatric service or assessment.

What about urgent or out-of-hours care?

This is not monitored. In the UK, use NHS 111 for urgent non-emergency help and call 999 for an emergency.
