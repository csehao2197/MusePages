001/ The idea

A good smart home is one nobody in the house notices.

The lights come up at the right level when you walk in, the heating knows the difference between a Tuesday and a Sunday, and nothing requires a phone. We design the control layer alongside your architect and electrician, before the walls close, which is the only time it is cheap to do properly.

004/ Stages

Stage 2

Concept.

We join the first design meeting and mark up the drawings before anything is priced.

Stage 4

Technical design.

Cable routes, keypad positions and a schedule your electrician can actually build from.

Stage 5

First fix.

We supervise the cabling on site. This is the stage that decides whether the rest goes well.

Stage 5

Second fix and programming.

Keypads on, scenes set, then a week of living with it before we adjust.

Handover

Handover.

A printed plan, every password, and a two hour walk through with whoever runs the house.
