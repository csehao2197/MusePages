FAQ

Good questions, honest answers

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend 4-6 weeks for private events, 3-6 months for weddings.

Do you provide staff and equipment?

Yes, full-service catering includes servers, bartenders, linens, and tableware.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. We offer vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, kosher, and halal options.

What is your cancellation policy?

Full refund 30+ days out, 50% refund 14-29 days, no refund under 14 days.

Do you offer tastings?

Complimentary tasting for events over 75 guests.
