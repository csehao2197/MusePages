Let's talk about your event →
How far in advance should I book?

We recommend 4–6 weeks for private events and 3–6 months for weddings, especially during popular seasons.

Do you provide staff and equipment?

Yes. Full-service catering includes servers, bartenders, linens, and tableware. We'll discuss the setup and service details during consultation.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. We offer vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, kosher, and halal options. Share dietary and certification requirements in your inquiry so they can be discussed individually.

What is your cancellation policy?

Our sample policy is a full refund 30 or more days out, a 50% refund 14–29 days out, and no refund under 14 days. This demonstration accepts no bookings or payments.

Do you offer tastings?

Complimentary tastings are offered for events over 75 guests in this sample service offering.
