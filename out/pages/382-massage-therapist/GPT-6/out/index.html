A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Common questions.

How should I prepare?

For a real appointment, ask the provider about arrival time, clothing, draping and preparation. Your comfort and consent should guide the session.

What if I have a medical condition?

Massage is not medical diagnosis or treatment. Discuss suitability with a qualified clinician and the provider privately. This demo does not collect health information or promise safety or results.
