OBSERVE BEFORE YOU CONCLUDE

Local signals. Not winners.

Only this browser records preview views, CTA clicks, form starts/completions, active visible seconds, scroll depth and relative canvas clicks. No form values are recorded. Selecting a variant creates another local view. Switching variants, hiding the page or exporting flushes the current session.

*Bounce: finalized local views with no recorded engagement. Abandonment: finalized form starts without completion. A local demo completion is not a real business conversion. Refresh to see persisted data.

01 / CONTROL

Establish a real baseline.

A is a proposed control, not a proven best performer. Historical benchmark data has not been supplied.

02 / RUN

Two weeks is a minimum.

Use a real randomized visitor split, consistent assets and tracking, mobile/desktop parity and at least 100 conversions per variant.

03 / DECIDE

Significance is a gate.

Predefine a primary metric and sample-size plan. Run a valid significance analysis before declaring a winner. Version E is only a combined hypothesis.
