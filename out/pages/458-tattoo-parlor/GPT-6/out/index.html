Find your language

Our styles

Fine Line

Delicate, single-needle precision work. Botanicals, portraits, script, and minimalist geometric — built for clients who want something subtle but lasting.

Traditional

Bold outlines, solid fills, timeless subjects. American traditional tattooing done the way it was meant to — confident lines, strong colour, built to age beautifully.

Blackwork

Statement pieces in solid black ink. Large-scale geometric, tribal-inspired, and architectural designs that command attention and hold their power over time.

Realism

Photorealistic portraits, nature, and objects rendered in black & grey or full colour. These illustrative flash studies show subjects, not evidence of tattoo technique.

The process

01

Consultation

Every piece starts here. We talk through your idea, placement, size, and references. No design work begins until we both feel aligned on the vision.

02

Design

Your artist creates a custom design based on the consultation. You'll receive a preview for review and revision before your session is booked.

03

Session

Come prepared, fed, and rested. Your artist will walk you through the stencil placement before any ink touches skin. We don't rush.

We provide a full written aftercare guide and remain available for questions during your healing period. A well-healed tattoo is a good tattoo.

Before the first line

FAQ

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes. The concept studio is appointment only, with no walk-ins. Supplied lead time is 4–8 weeks; actual availability is not checked here.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Pricing depends on size, placement, complexity and artist. The supplied minimum is £150. Larger custom work needs a consultation quote. No payment is accepted here.

Do you require a deposit?

The supplied concept policy requests a non-refundable deposit toward the total cost, lost for cancellations within 48 hours. This is not a live contract; confirm written terms with an actual operator before paying.

What should I do to prepare for my session?

Eat beforehand, stay hydrated and wear suitable clothing. The supplied brief recommends avoiding alcohol the night before and postponing work on irritated or sunburnt skin. Discuss medications, health concerns and suitability with qualified professionals directly, not this form.

Will it hurt?

Yes, to varying degrees depending on placement. Ask the artist for breaks and discuss concerns before proceeding. No comfort or clinical outcome can be guaranteed.

Can I bring someone with me?

The concept policy allows one seated, quiet support person. Confirm current studio arrangements directly.
