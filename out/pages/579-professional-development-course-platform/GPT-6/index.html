A few things you might be wondering

How do I choose my first course?

Start with one skill that would make this week better. Use the topic, level, and coach filters, or choose “Find your next step” to set your interests and weekly learning target.

Are coaching sessions and certificates real?

This is a local interactive demonstration. Profiles, coaching availability, enrollment, payments, community, and badges are illustrative. No coach receives your request, no payment is taken, and badges are not accredited credentials.

Where is my progress stored?

Your learning, goals, saved resources, and preferences are stored only in this browser on this device. No account is created. Clearing browser storage removes this data. Do not enter sensitive personal information.
