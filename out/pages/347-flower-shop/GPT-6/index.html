OUR STORY

Born from a love
of flowers —
and their moments.

Wild Bloom Co. imagines a small studio at the edge of a market district: a borrowed van, a workbench full of stems, and an obsession with arrangements that feel like something.

No generic gestures. Just the right stems, palette and proportions. This fictional studio's ambition is local sourcing where possible, seasonality always.

MADE WITH CARE. GIVEN WITH JOY.

For a little longer.

Fresh water, clean vase.

Trim stems, remove submerged leaves and refresh water regularly.

A cool place to bloom.

Keep flowers away from radiators, harsh sunlight and ripening fruit.

A note for pet people.

Lilies and other stems may be toxic to pets. Keep flowers out of reach; check species with a real florist.
