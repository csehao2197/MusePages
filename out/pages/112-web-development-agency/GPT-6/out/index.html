Notes from the build.

Choosing a boring architecture

Start with clear domain boundaries, an observable monolith and a small deployment surface. Add distribution only when the constraints justify its cost.

Accessibility is an engineering concern

Semantic markup, predictable focus and keyboard-operable controls are product foundations, not decorative polish.

Open source, openly considered

We value documented fixes and reproducible examples. This independent concept does not claim public contributions or link to invented repositories.
