THE SECTION THAT MATTERS

FULL FACE.
CLEAR RULES.
PROPER BRIEFING.

This is a sports venue concept, not a verified safety operation. Confirm current standards, protective equipment, staffing, insurance and suitability directly with the operator.

Protection and supervision

The brief proposes full-face thermal masks, supervised equipment testing and one first-aid-trained marshal per eight players. Those claims and the cited EN166 equipment compliance are unverified here. Follow the actual operator’s safety briefing and equipment instructions at all times.

Age and consent

Minimum twelve in the concept. Ages twelve to fifteen use a separate lower-velocity junior programme. Under-eighteens need the operator’s actual parent/guardian consent process; this page is not a waiver.

Kit inclusions

The brief’s package list and general kit section differ on glove and armour inclusions. Confirm overalls, gloves, neck protection, armour and suitable mask fit before paying. No injury-free guarantee is offered.

Weather and comfort

Wear suitable footwear and follow the operator’s weather decisions. No claim that all weather is safe. Ask qualified staff about accessibility, eyewear and individual suitability; this page does not provide medical advice.
