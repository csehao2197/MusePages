07 / LET'S CLEAR THINGS UP

GOT QUESTIONS?
GOOD. WE LIKE THAT.

Start where you are.
Know what you're getting into.

Do I need any equipment?+

The concept is bodyweight-first. Resistance bands and dumbbells are optional variations; use only equipment you can use safely.

What if I miss a day?+

In the imagined program, workouts can be made up within 48 hours and streaks pause. In this demo, all progress resets when the page reloads.

I'm a total beginner — is this for me?+

Explore beginner, intermediate, and beast-mode concepts. Choose a comfortable variation, take breaks, and seek qualified guidance for your individual needs.

How long are the workouts?+

The samples run 20–35 minutes. The practice timer demonstrates a 20-second interval, not a complete guided workout or video.

Is there a diet plan?+

There's a downloadable sample meal framework, not a rigid diet plan or personalized macro prescription. Adapt meals to your needs and dietary requirements.

When does the challenge start?+

The original concept date is April 1, 2026, which has passed. This is an archived design demo with no scheduled live cohort. The countdown stays at zero.
