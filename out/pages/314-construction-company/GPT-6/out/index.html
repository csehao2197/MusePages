HOW A JOB RUNS

FROM BID
TO KEYS.

No mystery. No disappearing acts.
Open each phase to see what happens next.

01 BID & AWARD 2–6 WEEKS

We review documents, walk the site, and price it honestly. If budget and drawings disagree, you hear before award.

02 PRECONSTRUCTION 4–10 WEEKS

Constructability, long-lead procurement, permit strategy and a schedule built backwards from occupancy.

03 MOBILISATION 1–2 WEEKS

Site logistics, safety planning and temporary utilities. Your superintendent stays from day one to closeout.

04 CONSTRUCTION VARIES BY SCOPE

Weekly owner-architect-contractor meetings, a live schedule and change orders priced within five business days.

05 CLOSEOUT 3–5 WEEKS

Punch list, commissioning, as-builts, O&M manuals and facilities-team training before keys change hands.

06 WARRANTY 12 MONTHS MINIMUM

A one-year warranty walk at month eleven. A phone number that still works at month thirteen.
