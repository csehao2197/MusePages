A note from the writer

Hi, I’m Nora.
Curious by default.

I’m a fictional writer and editor with a soft spot for good questions. I started The Good Edit as an antidote to the feeling of being informed about everything and interested in nothing.

The premise is simple: we don’t need more information. We need a few things worth paying attention to.

See you Tuesday, Nora

A few good questions.

How often will I hear from you?

The concept is one letter every Tuesday, designed to read in about five minutes. This static demo sends no email.

Is it really free?

Yes, the proposed newsletter is free. There is no checkout or payment collection in this demo.

Can I unsubscribe?

A live service would include an unsubscribe link in every letter. This preview creates no subscription to cancel.

What email platform do you use?

No email platform is connected. The signup form validates your email and shows a local preview only.

What happens to my email here?

It stays in the page while you preview the form. It is not sent to a server or saved to device storage.
