THE BUILDING

Built for careful work.

The Bindery was a bookbinding works from 1891 until 1987. The floor still has bolt holes where the sewing frames stood. The windows face north, which is why the bookbinders chose it and why the light is even all day. We added heating, wiring and better chairs. The room did the rest.
