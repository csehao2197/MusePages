NOTES FROM THE FLOOR

The tide journal.

A little context before your first rep.
Short, general information — not medical advice.

01 / FIRST DAYWhat to expect

A typical concept class starts with a welcome, warm-up, and movement briefing, followed by skill practice, a scaled workout, and a cool-down. Wear comfortable clothing and bring water. Discuss individual needs with a qualified coach.

02 / THE WHITEBOARDRx, scaled & you

“Rx” refers to the written workout. “Scaled” means adapting the movement, load, or volume. Neither is a measure of your worth. The sample WOD is not a personal prescription; get appropriate guidance.

03 / EVERYDAY BASICSKeep it simple

Make room for rest, comfortable movement, regular meals, and water. Nutrition needs vary; personalized advice belongs with an appropriately qualified professional. This site offers no diet plan or supplement recommendations.
