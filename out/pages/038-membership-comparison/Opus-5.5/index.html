      Frequently asked questions

      Still curious? Talk to an advisor.

      Can I upgrade my membership?

      Yes, anytime from the app or front desk. Upgrades take effect immediately and you only pay the prorated difference for the current month.

      Is there a joining fee?

      No. There's no joining or enrollment fee on any plan, and your first month is free when you join today.

      What's the contract length?

      Monthly plans are month-to-month. Annual plans are paid up front for 12 months at the discounted rate and include the 30-day money-back guarantee.

      Can I freeze my membership?

      Yes. Freeze for up to 3 months per year for travel, injury or any reason, free of charge.

      Do you offer student/senior discounts?

      Students save 20% and seniors (65+) save 15% on any plan with valid ID. See all special offers below.

      Can I try before I join?

      Absolutely. Grab a free day pass below and try any area your chosen plan includes, plus one group class.
