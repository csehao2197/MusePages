LET’S CLEAR THINGS UP

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Everything you need for your next step.

Can I upgrade my membership?

In this fictional club, upgrades take effect at the start of your next billing month. Try any plan in the estimate here; this does not change a real membership.

Is there a joining fee?

The demo joining fee is $0. Add-ons are itemized separately. Estimates exclude any applicable taxes, which this static experience cannot calculate.

What’s the contract length?

Basic, Standard and Premium monthly plans have an illustrative 3-month initial term, then roll monthly. Annual plans prepay 12 months. Elite has no minimum term and may be canceled anytime; annual Elite refunds unused full months at the annual rate.

Can I freeze my membership?

The illustrative policy allows up to 3 months of freeze per year with 7 days’ notice. Billing and access pause; prepaid annual terms extend by the frozen period.

Do you offer student/senior discounts?

Yes, the demo offers students 20%, seniors aged 65+ 15%, and military or first responders 15% off base dues. Choose one in your estimate. Discounts combine with annual rates and the first-month credit; add-ons are not discounted. No documents are collected.

Can I try before I join?

Explore a free-day-pass or tour request preview below. This is a local demonstration; it issues no pass, reserves no visit and contacts no staff.
