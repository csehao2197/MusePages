A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING.

Good questions.
Honest answers.

A little clarity before the first bite.

Do I need to count calories?+

In the example coaching service, we handle the numbers. You just follow the plan and enjoy the food. A real coach would agree on an approach that is appropriate for you.

Can you work with food allergies?+

Plans would be built around your restrictions and preferences. This static sample is not allergy-safe advice; confirm ingredients, cross-contact risks and substitutions with a qualified professional.

How quickly will I see results?+

The brief suggests energy changes within 1 week and body composition changes within 4–6 weeks. Those are illustrative marketing timelines, not evidence or a guarantee. Individual experiences vary.

Can I still eat out at restaurants?+

Absolutely. The example Pro and Elite services include a restaurant dining guide. This demo does not include that guide, but your local assessment can note a busy lifestyle.

What if I don't like a meal on my plan?+

The proposed service lets you swap meals from a 200+ recipe library. That library is a concept feature, not an implemented service here. No foods need to be forced into your routine.
