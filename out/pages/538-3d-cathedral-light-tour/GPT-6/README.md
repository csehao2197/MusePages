## Limits

Lighting follows the prescribed solar-elevation and projection-length formulas, not physically validated spectral ray tracing. Colored floor meshes are flattened copies of original glass geometry with simplified geometric transformation; transparent additive transmission is illustrative. Dust and geometry are reduced on mobile, rendering pauses offscreen/hidden and reduced-motion uses discrete manual camera states. Glass and section diagrams remain geometric, not religious or historical imagery. Source attribution and the noncommercial license are retained.
