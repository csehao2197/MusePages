003/ Process

HOW A FILM
GETS MADE.

Week 1

We come and look.

A day on site with no camera, talking to the people who will be in it.

Week 2

A treatment, in writing.

Two pages: what the film is, who speaks, and what it will cost. No mood boards.

Week 3

Shoot.

Usually two days. We work around your shifts rather than the other way round.

Week 5

First cut.

One round of notes included, a second if the first changes the shape.

Week 7

Delivered.

Master, subtitled version, social crops and the raw files if you want them.
