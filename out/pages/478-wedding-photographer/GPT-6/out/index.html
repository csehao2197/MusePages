THE PERSON BEHIND THE CAMERA

Hi, I'm Clara.

I believe the photographs that matter most are the ones that bring you right back. Your father's hand on your shoulder. The wind catching your veil. The laughter just after the speeches.

I work with natural light and a gentle, observant approach, giving you space to be together rather than a list of poses to remember. The result is a story that feels like your day, not somebody else's idea of it.
