How we calculate your potential

Annual labor value = weekly task hours × participating people × hourly cost × 52. Modeled gross benefit = recovered labor value + replaceable tool cost. First-year net value subtracts a full year of Relay fees and $2,400 setup.

The assumptions behind the estimate

What ROI and payback actually mean

ROI = first-year net value ÷ (annual subscription + setup) × 100. Setup payback = setup ÷ (monthly gross benefit − monthly subscription), assuming monthly billing. If recurring net benefit is zero or negative, setup never pays back in this model.

What this calculator leaves out

Taxes, financing, inflation, discounts, learning curves and ongoing administration are excluded. Recovered hours are capacity, not guaranteed cash savings. Accuracy improvements have no extra dollar value here, so time is counted once. All plans and scenarios are fictional, with no measured industry benchmarks or customer case studies.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s clear things up.

Useful numbers start with honest context.

How accurate is this calculator?

It is a transparent planning model, not a forecast or quote. Try low and high inputs and compare the conservative and ambitious scenarios. Your actual time recovery and costs will vary.

What data do you use?

Your inputs and explicit fictional planning assumptions. Industry changes the recovery assumption, not a measured peer average. Logos, plans and example teams are fictional.

Is my information secure?

Calculations and contact forms run locally. Saved estimates use this browser’s local storage only after you choose Save, and contain numeric inputs, industry and goals—not contact details or free text. Shared URLs include the same non-contact inputs. Your host may keep normal access logs.

How do I get started?

Complete the three steps, review the first-year cost and try different inputs. You can print to PDF, download an HTML report, save locally or create a clearly labeled sample trial preview. Demo planning creates a personal calendar reminder, not a booked meeting.
