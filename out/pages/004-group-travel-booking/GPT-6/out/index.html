A few things you're wondering

Big plans. Clear answers.

What's the minimum group size?

Proposed standard packages start at 6 travelers, and corporate retreats at 10. Our planning tool supports groups up to 50.

Can we customize the itinerary?

Absolutely. In a real booking flow, your coordinator would help tailor activities, stays, and the pace of every day. Try the local voting demo above.

How does split payment work?

The proposed service sends each member an individual payment link, with the option to pay in full or in three installments. No payment processing exists on this concept page.

What if someone drops out?

The concept offers flexible cancellation up to 30 days before departure with a full refund. This is illustrative, not a binding offer; actual terms would be confirmed before a booking.

Do you handle flights?

Proposed packages can include arranged flights or let everyone book independently. Calculator estimates here exclude flights.

Is travel insurance included?

Travel insurance would be optional at checkout. No insurance product or checkout is connected here.
