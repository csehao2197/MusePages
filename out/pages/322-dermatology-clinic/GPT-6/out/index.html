PATIENT QUESTIONS

Clear answers start here.

How soon can I be seen?

Availability requires direct confirmation. This demo has no live appointments. For a changing lesion, contact a qualified healthcare professional promptly.

Will insurance cover my visit?

Confirm your benefits and network status with your insurer and chosen practice.

Do I need a referral?

Requirements differ by plan and service; ask your insurer before booking.

What happens after an examination?

Your clinician explains any recommended tests, treatment and follow-up based on the findings.

Can I discuss both types of care?

Ask your clinic whether combined visits are available and how separate fees are handled.

Can children have a dermatology visit?

Ask about pediatric services, guardian consent and age-appropriate preparation.
