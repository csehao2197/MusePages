A MOMENT BEFORE THE CLIMB

A little less force.
A little more timing.

A short practice for players who enjoy timing-based action games. Walk the Rainveil Pass, calibrate three mechanical guardians, then enter the summit circle.

Move
WASD / arrows, click the trail, or the touch pad.
Strike
J, click a guardian, or Strike. Land three close-range hits within a rhythm to finish a form.
Dodge
Space or Dodge. The red ground ring warns of an incoming sweep. Dodge as it closes.
Breathe
Strikes use 12 Breath; dodges use 24. Step away to recover. Three lost composure marks restart only the current lesson.
