COMMON QUESTIONS

A little clarity, right here.

Do I need an accountant if I use accounting software?

Software records transactions; an accountant helps interpret them, review compliance and plan ahead.

What is the difference between bookkeeping and accounting?

Bookkeeping records daily transactions. Accounting uses those records to prepare accounts and understand performance.

When do I need to register for VAT?

Registration depends on taxable turnover and current HMRC rules. Check official guidance or request advice for your circumstances.

How quickly can you take over?

Timing depends on records and your current accountant. An introductory call helps define a sensible handover.
