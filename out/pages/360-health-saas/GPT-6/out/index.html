Before you ask

Clear about
the limits.

This is a working interface, not a working health service. There are no live models, patient records or clinical validation studies behind it.

Is my health data secure here?

This page accepts no real health records. Sample connections are local state only. It makes no HIPAA, SOC 2, encryption-service or certification claim. Real deployment would require independent security, privacy and legal review.

Can I upload a lab report?

No. Use the synthetic lab-history view. A production parser and direct lab connections are outside this static demonstration.

Which wearables can I connect?

The names in the sample catalog illustrate a possible product direction. Buttons simulate connection states without permissions, credentials or provider APIs.

How accurate is the AI analysis?

There is no AI analysis running here. Notes are scripted and based on synthetic values. No accuracy percentage, clinical study, diagnostic performance or medical credential is claimed.

Can I share a report with my clinician?

The demo exports synthetic CSV and JSON files only. They are clearly marked demo data and are not clinical reports. A real product would need an appropriate review and sharing workflow.

What is retained?

All settings, sample connections and form previews stay in this page's memory and reset on reload. No data is submitted or stored by the page. A file you explicitly download stays under your control.
