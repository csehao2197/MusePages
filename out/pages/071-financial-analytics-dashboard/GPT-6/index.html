GOOD QUESTIONS

A little healthy
skepticism.

Is my financial information uploaded?

No. Inputs stay in this tab's memory. There is no analytics beacon, model endpoint, bank link or server connection. Downloaded snapshots contain your entered data; store and share them intentionally. Refreshing clears the local state.

Does the allocation score measure real investment risk?

No. It is an illustrative weighted asset-class index, not volatility, VaR, future performance or suitability. The label-level concentration calculation cannot detect underlying holdings or sector overlap. Neither value is an investment recommendation.

Are the trades real?

No. LAB/USD is fictional. Both sides are seeded or locally entered; no exchange, funds, positions, fees, settlement or anti-self-trade controls exist. The matching algorithm itself is functional.

Is the full-stack and AI platform connected?

This is an independent static homepage and local educational playground. Next.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, authentication and OpenAI/LLM services are not implemented or connected here. No CV claims of production infrastructure or AI integration are made.
