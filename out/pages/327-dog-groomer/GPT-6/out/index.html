A FEW FRIENDLY ANSWERS

Good questions.

How long does a groom take?

A full groom may take around 2–3 hours, depending on size, coat and individual comfort. Confirm timing with the salon.

Do you use cages for drying?

The concept is cage-free: supervised towels and suitable low-heat drying. Ask a real salon about its handling arrangements.

My dog is anxious. Can I enquire?

Yes. Discuss behaviour, triggers and handling needs in advance. A meet-and-greet may help assess suitability; outcomes vary.

How often should I book?

Many coats need regular maintenance. Ask a groomer for a schedule suited to the coat and lifestyle; matting may require a different approach.
