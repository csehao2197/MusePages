Our Story

From seed to cup, we know every hand that touches your coffee

Ember & Bloom was born in a 200-square-foot garage in 2018, when our founder Maya Chen started roasting beans she'd sourced directly from a women's cooperative in Huila, Colombia. Today, we partner with 12 farming communities across Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and Indonesia — paying 40% above fair-trade prices.

Every batch is roasted in small 15-kilo lots at our Pearl District roastery, profiled to bring out the unique character of each origin. No dark-roast shortcuts. No commodity blends. Just honest, traceable coffee.
