BEFORE YOU VISIT

Thoughtful answers.

Does acupuncture hurt?

Experiences vary. Some people report brief discomfort or aching. Tell your practitioner if anything feels uncomfortable.

How many sessions will I need?

There is no guaranteed number or outcome. Discuss goals, evidence, cost and a review point before committing.

Can I continue conventional care?

Keep your healthcare team informed. This page does not recommend replacing prescribed treatment. Herbs may interact with medicines.

What about pregnancy or medical conditions?

Discuss pregnancy, bleeding disorders, medications and other relevant health information with your clinician before treatment.
