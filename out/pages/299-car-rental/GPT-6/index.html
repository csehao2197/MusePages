THE QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK

Good to know before you go.

Can I hire at 21?

Economy and Compact classes only, including Compact Automatic in this demo, with £14/day young-driver surcharge until 25. Under 21 cannot hire.

Can I add another driver?

One is included. Each additional driver beyond that is £8/day and must be present at pickup with their licence.

Do you do one-way hires?

Between our three branches for £45. Elsewhere, no. One-way journeys over 300 miles require an additional quote.

What deposit do you take?

A pre-authorisation equal to the selected excess on the main driver's credit card. Normally released within five working days.

What happens if I damage it?

Compare pickup photographs, assess the repair and charge up to your excess, subject to cover terms. The sample policy adds no separate repair administration fee.

Can I take it abroad?

Ireland with fourteen days' notice and a £60 documentation fee. Continental Europe is not permitted by the sample policy.
