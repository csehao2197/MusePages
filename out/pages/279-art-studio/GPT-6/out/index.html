EIGHT AT A TIME

Eight places, one room above a carpet warehouse, and a tutor who will not tell you that you have a good eye.

We teach oil and acrylic to people who have never held a brush and to people who painted at school in 1974 and stopped. Nobody shows anybody their phone photographs of the Lake District.

A fictional Manchester studio concept. All work and room imagery are original illustrations.
