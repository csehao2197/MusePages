# Writing to persuade
Grade 6 · English · Foundation
Curriculum: School curriculum · review required
2 sessions × 60 minutes · Focus: Whole-child learning

Curated, deterministic teaching template. Adapt to your class and verify local curriculum alignment.

10 items · 6 foundation (60%) / 3 improve (30%) / 1 challenge (10%). Suggested time: 20–30 minutes, not part of the 60-minute sessions.

## Foundation / required
1. Write a classroom-related claim.
2. Give a reason supporting your claim.
3. Name your intended audience.
4. Suggest evidence you could actually collect.
5. Explain why invented statistics are unreliable.
6. Write a concluding sentence.

## Improve / optional
7. Revise an opinion into a reasoned argument.
8. Explain how one detail supports your claim.
9. Write a fair counterargument and response.

## Challenge / optional
10. Adapt your paragraph to a different audience and justify your choices.

## Reference answers & reasoning
Check after attempting the questions. Open-ended answers may vary; reasoning must be accurate.
1. A clear arguable position, e.g. We should have a daily reading break.
2. A relevant reason, e.g. It protects time for reading.
3. A specific audience, e.g. classmates or the headteacher.
4. A real class survey or reading log; do not present proposed data as collected.
5. They cannot be verified and can mislead readers.
6. Restate the position with a clear, proportionate call to action.
7. Include a claim and a logically linked reason; wording varies.
8. Explain the logical connection rather than merely repeating the detail.
9. Accurately represent an opposing view and offer a respectful, reasoned reply.
10. Accept justified changes in tone, vocabulary and evidence appropriate to the new audience.
