A LITTLE GUIDANCE

From a starting point
to your next great lesson.

  1. Choose a topic, learner group and learning focus.
  2. Create a structured plan: two 60-minute sessions, or three for advanced learners.
  3. Switch documents, edit the text and export Markdown files. Edits save locally after each change.

Curated examples cover fractions, the water cycle and persuasive writing. Other topics use a reusable, explicitly generic scaffold. No model inference, curriculum verification or external AI service is provided.

Drafts stay in this browser's local storage. Do not enter sensitive student information. Clearing browser data removes drafts. Exports are your portable backup.
