Our community guidelines

Focus on the item and your experience. Use respectful language and protect personal information. This demo keeps submissions in your tab only; a production store would add moderation and purchase verification.

What makes a helpful review?

Tell readers how long you used the item, what you carried, which size you chose and what worked or could improve. Include specific details rather than only a star rating.

Photos & video tips

Use your own images with clear lighting. The local demo accepts up to three PNG/JPEG/WebP images (5MB each, at most 4096×4096) or MP4/WebM videos (20MB each, at most 30 seconds and 1920×1920). Media previews stay in memory.

Incentives & disclosure

Disclose gifts, discounts or other incentives in your review. Our demo records the disclosure label you choose; it never equates an incentive or a simulated order match with independent purchase verification.
