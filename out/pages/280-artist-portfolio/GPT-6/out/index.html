The artist

About the work.

Yuki Nakamura (b. 1986, Tokyo) is a painter working between Tokyo and Berlin. Her work explores the resistance of material — the way oil paint holds and refuses and surprises — and what happens when representation is taken to the point of its own dissolution.

She holds an MFA from the Städelschule, Frankfurt (2014) and has exhibited internationally since 2016.

Represented in this fictional portfolio by Sprüth Magers (Berlin/London) and STANDARD (OSLO).

“The point at which a form stops being recognisable. Where does the figure end and the field begin?”
