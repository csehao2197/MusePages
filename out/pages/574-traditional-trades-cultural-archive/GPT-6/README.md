## Implementation

Source: this directory's `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`, and `images\`. Static export: `out\`. Final subpath: `/pages/traditional-trades-cultural-archive/GPT-6/index.html`.

The cinematic black, cream and gold design uses original woodcut-style tool plates and a hand-illustrated lamplit workshop. Twelve individual trade profiles include imagined workshop vignettes, romanized names, tools, city associations and synthesized sound impressions. Search, craft filters, keyboard-accessible story dialogs, 15 interactive atlas stops, a 12-trade comparison slider, validated interest-form demonstration, local visit counter and downloadable illustrated field notes work without a backend.

The site translates titles, interface and stories into English; Chinese calligraphy is replaced with English serif typography and an abstract VT maker's mark. Trade names also have plain Latin romanization. No Chinese characters or invented authentic inscriptions are displayed.

## Explicit adaptations and limits

- This authorized static adaptation uses HTML/CSS/browser JavaScript rather than React/TypeScript/Vite, Tailwind, shadcn, Framer Motion or a runtime router. CSS provides grain, shimmer, hover, scroll and dialog transitions with reduced-motion support. One page has the requested route data attributes. Header/footer remain outside main.
- The original brief requested generated documentary-style photographs and historical image pairs. Original SVG illustrations are used instead, with no claim to be real documentary imagery. Comparison scenes show conceptual contexts of the same original tool plate, not ten-year historical photographs. The download is a single original SVG field-notes sheet, not a purported documentary asset ZIP.
- All stories are explicitly fictional editorial vignettes. City associations come from the creative brief, not field research. The extra three atlas stops are open research stops. No 28-person research sample, verified artisan addresses, dates or uniform extinction claims are invented.
- Sound is locally synthesized with trade-specific oscillator/filter/rhythm configurations, not field audio. Hover playback is opt-in to respect autoplay and accessibility expectations; dialog playback works on explicit activation. Volume is limited and each sound ends automatically.
- No EdgeOne deployment, skill installation, Edge Functions, KV service, API request, external AI or build is performed. No platform sponsorship/hosting claim is shown. Visits count only in this browser's localStorage. The form validates inputs, immediately clears them, and shows a local confirmation; it sends/stores no name or phone. No real apprenticeship application is created.
- Atlas geography is a labeled schematic, not an authoritative boundary map. The page is an exploration concept, not a scholarly or practical craft/safety reference.

Private QA entry: `node out\pages\574-traditional-trades-cultural-archive\GPT-6\.checks\verify.mjs`. It uses installed Playwright Chromium and the existing preview server at the final public subpath. Owned browsers and preview servers were closed.

Both real desktop/mobile PNGs and story/archive screenshots were opened for visual review. Refinements corrected card alignment, strengthened story observations, fixed modern-browser phone-pattern escaping, and improved narrow-screen type. Final evidence is in `.checks\qa.json`; standard preview generation and its own image review are tracked there separately.
