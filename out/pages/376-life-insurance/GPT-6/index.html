01 / COVER

What we arrange.

Level term.
A fixed amount for a fixed number of years. Often considered for family protection. Suitability and price depend on the policy and your circumstances.
Decreasing term.
Cover reduces over time and may be considered alongside a repayment mortgage. Check that the schedule and terms match your needs.
Family income benefit.
Pays an income for the remaining policy term rather than one lump sum, subject to the policy terms. It is worth discussing how your family would use it.
Critical illness.
Pays on diagnosis of a condition that meets the policy definition. Exclusions, severity criteria and exact wording matter. It does not cover every illness.

03 / THE PROCESS

How we work.

  1. 01

    The call. A proposed twenty minute conversation about what you owe, who depends on you and what you already have through work.

  2. 02

    The research. Review suitable options and explain the reasons in writing. The scope of any market review should be confirmed with your actual broker.

  3. 03

    The application. Answer medical questions fully and honestly, including the awkward ones. Inaccurate or incomplete answers can affect cover or a claim. Ask for clarification if unsure.

  4. 04

    The review. Revisit cover when your mortgage, job or family changes, and agree any review arrangement with your broker.
