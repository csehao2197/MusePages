    About Maya

    I'm a freelance illustrator based in Portland, Oregon, creating work that blends the natural world with human emotion. Gardens at midnight, birds on the move, a single lit window in a sleeping city — I'm drawn to quiet moments that hold a bigger story.

    I've been illustrating professionally for more than eight years, after studying Illustration at the Rhode Island School of Design. I work mostly in Procreate and Photoshop, starting every piece with pencil thumbnails and building texture by hand.

    My clients have included The New Yorker, Bloomberg, HarperCollins, Oatly and Wired, alongside independent publishers, coffee roasters and small studios who care about craft as much as I do.
