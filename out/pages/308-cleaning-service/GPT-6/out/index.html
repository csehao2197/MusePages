GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

What does a standard clean include?

The sample scope includes bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen surfaces and living areas. Appliances, heavy lifting and specialist stains need separate agreement.

Do I need to be home?

Discuss secure access directly with your provider. This form does not ask for keys, access codes or a street address.

Are supplies included?

Confirm products and equipment before the visit. No product can be assumed safe for every surface, pet or sensitivity.

How is the estimate calculated?

Home services start with one bedroom and add $20 per extra bedroom. Office adds $30 per extra 500 sq ft unit. Carpet is $75 per room; windows $60 per five-window set. Recurring sample discounts apply to the base only; extras are added afterward.

Can I cancel or change a visit?

Confirm the actual provider’s cancellation and rescheduling terms in writing. This demonstration makes no booking and charges no cancellation fee.

Does this form book a cleaner?

No. It creates a temporary summary on your screen. Nothing is transmitted, reserved or charged.
