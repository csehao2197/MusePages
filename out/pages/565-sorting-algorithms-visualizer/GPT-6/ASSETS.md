# Assets

All visuals are drawn in code: canvas 2D stages, an original three-bar SVG logo/favicon, and Lucide SVG control glyphs.

- `@fontsource-variable/bricolage-grotesque`: Bricolage Grotesque, weights 600/700, SIL OFL-1.1.
- `@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans`: IBM Plex Sans, weights 400/500/600, SIL OFL-1.1.
- `@fontsource/jetbrains-mono`: JetBrains Mono, weights 400/500/700, SIL OFL-1.1.
- `lucide-react` from the existing repository installation: eight glyphs extracted as browser-native inline SVG, ISC (including the package's Feather MIT notices).

Only Latin WOFF2 subsets are shipped. Full copyright and license notices are in `licenses\` and the public export. Fonts are local, with no CDN requests. No photographs, third-party screenshots, or external image assets are used.
