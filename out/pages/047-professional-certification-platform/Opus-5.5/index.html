Common Questions

Are CredPath certifications recognized by employers?

Yes. Over 500 companies actively use CredPath credentials in their hiring process, and every credential can be verified instantly through our employer portal or API.

How long does it take to prepare?

Most candidates complete study in 6-8 weeks studying 30-45 minutes per day. Foundational certifications take less; expert tracks can take longer.

What happens if I don't pass the exam?

All-Access members get unlimited retakes. Single cert purchasers get one free retake if they complete 100% of the study path before their first attempt.

Are exams proctored?

Yes. Exams are online-proctored with flexible scheduling 7 days a week, 6am–midnight in your timezone. You only need a webcam and a stable connection.

Can my employer pay for my certification?

Yes. We offer invoicing, purchase orders, and tax-deductible receipts. Many employers also reimburse through L&D budgets.

Do certifications expire?

Certifications are valid for 3 years. Renewal requires a shorter assessment and continuing education credits.
