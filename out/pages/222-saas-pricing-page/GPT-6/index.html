Can I change plans later?

Of course. Upgrade at any time with a prorated charge. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

The full product accepts major credit and debit cards. Enterprise teams can arrange annual invoicing or bank transfer. This demo collects no payment information.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Explore Pro for 14 days with no credit card. At the end, choose Pro or continue on Free; your work stays accessible within Free limits.

What happens when I exceed my limits?

We notify you before you reach a limit. Additional project creation or uploads pause until you upgrade or reduce usage; existing work remains available. Pro supports up to 20 users.

Do you offer refunds?

Contact us within 30 days of your first payment for a refund review. Local consumer rights apply. Annual plans renew yearly, with reminders before renewal.
