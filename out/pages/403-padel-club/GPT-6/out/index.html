THE RULES FIT
ON A NAPKIN.

  1. Always doubles. Four players, two a side.
  2. Serve underarm, below the waist, into the diagonally opposite box.
  3. The ball can bounce once, then hit the glass and stay in play.
  4. You can play it off your own back wall too.
  5. Score like tennis: 15, 30, 40, game.
  6. Start wherever you are. There is room to learn.
