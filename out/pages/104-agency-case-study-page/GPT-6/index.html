The challenge

A wild spirit.
A website standing still.

Rewild made thoughtful gear for people who love being outside. But a fragmented identity and a cluttered shopping journey were getting in the way of that simple purpose.

Our brief: create one clear story across brand and digital. Make choosing the right gear feel less like a transaction, and more like the beginning of an adventure. Accessibility, mobile performance and a fixed seasonal launch shaped every decision.

The approach

Start with the trail.
Not the product.

We put the experience outside at the centre of everything.

Interviews with twelve sample customers and a review of the shopping journey pointed to the same need: confidence. We organised the experience around adventures, simplified product comparisons and gave the brand an honest, welcoming voice.

Weekly working sessions kept the client close to every decision. A shared prototype made trade-offs visible early.

Six stages. One shared direction.

From first question to final detail.

Weeks 1–2 / Discover

Customer interviews, content audit and an accessibility baseline.

Weeks 3–4 / Define

Positioning, journey mapping and a clear adventure-first architecture.

Weeks 5–7 / Design

An earthy visual system, expressive type and mobile prototypes.

Weeks 8–12 / Build

Semantic components, image budgets and keyboard-first navigation.

Weeks 13–15 / Refine

Usability sessions, cross-device checks and performance tuning.

Week 16 / Launch plan

A documented handover and measurement plan. This concept is not live.
