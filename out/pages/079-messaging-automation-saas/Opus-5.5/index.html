    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Which accounts work with Trigly?

    Instagram Professional accounts (Creator or Business) linked to a Facebook Page. Personal accounts can't receive automated DMs through the official API.

    Will my replies look automated?

    Static replies say exactly what you write. Smart AI replies follow your brief, use the follower's message for context, and stay under two sentences.

    What happens if two automations match?

    The first active automation in your list wins, so put the specific ones (tied to a post) above general ones.

    Can I pause everything?

    Yes. Each automation has its own on/off switch, and disconnecting your account stops all replies immediately.
