What this edition actually does.

Capture text you paste, search and group it, inspect shared-tag connections, and schedule real SM-2 reviews using UTC calendar days. Original sample passages are fictional demonstration content.

Summaries are the first two sentences, tags follow fixed keywords, digests quote sources grouped by shared tags, and chat retrieves matching notes. None of these are AI outputs.
