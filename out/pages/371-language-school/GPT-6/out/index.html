GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity before you begin.

What level do I need?

The example programmes cover absolute beginners through advanced learners, with ranges varying by language. The confidence check is a self-reflection, not a formal placement test.

How are lessons delivered?

The concept is designed around online teaching. This static page has no video platform, teacher matching, shared notes or actual lessons.

Is a trial really free?

The brief describes a free group trial and a £15 individual 45-minute trial. The local planner distinguishes these; it does not reserve a place or charge a fee.

Can I switch teachers or cancel?

A real school must confirm matching, availability, cancellation terms and refunds before booking. This concept opens no account and holds no sessions.
