05 / YOUR FIRST TEN MINUTES

NEVER TRAINED?
START HERE.

You don’t have to arrive confident. A good first class gives you room to learn, ask questions and set a comfortable pace.

What do I wear?

The brief suggests shorts and a t-shirt with loaner equipment. Confirm suitable clothing, hygiene rules and gear availability with the real academy before attending.

What happens in class one?

The proposed welcome starts ten minutes early: a coach introduces the space and explains the class. A first session focuses on controlled drills, not sparring.

Do I need to be fit first?

Beginners are welcome at their current level. Discuss relevant needs privately with a qualified coach, and seek medical guidance if you have concerns about exercising.

Will I get hurt?

All physical training carries injury risk. Supervision, optional sparring and respecting boundaries can reduce risk but do not eliminate it. No safety guarantee is offered.
