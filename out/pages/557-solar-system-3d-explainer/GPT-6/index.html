A note on the model & the numbers

This is a geometric teaching model, not a navigation tool. Orbits are circular and coplanar at mean distances; phases are composed, not today’s sky. Spin rates and tilts are included. True scale uses one ratio for diameters and orbital distances, without minimum visible planet sizes. The live pixel figure is a perspective projection at the current camera distance. Readable mode deliberately compresses distances and enlarges planets independently. The star field and procedural surfaces are original illustrations, not photographs. Reduced-motion mode makes camera transitions immediate.

Rounded reference figures: NASA planetary fact sheets. Values are approximate mean diameters, mean Sun distances, sidereal orbits and sidereal rotations. No live data or external requests.
