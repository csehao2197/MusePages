    You will hear from us every evening

    KEEPING EVERYONE WELL

    Before their first stay.

    Vaccinations

    Up to date DHP and leptospirosis, plus kennel cough at least two weeks before arrival. We check the card on drop-off, every time, no exceptions.

    Flea and worm treatment

    Within the last month. We can treat on arrival for £12 if you have forgotten.

    Trial day for first-timers

    Every new dog does a free half-day before their first overnight stay. It tells us how they cope and it tells you whether you are comfortable.

    Neutering

    Required for stays over fourteen nights, and for any dog over eight months sharing a paddock group.
