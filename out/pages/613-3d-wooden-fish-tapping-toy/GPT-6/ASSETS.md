# Authorship and licenses

- All sculpture geometry, carved mouth and face lobes, scalloped tail, routed ridges, padded mallet, stone plinth, architectural fins, dust, ripple and particles are original procedural Three.js geometry in `src\app.js`.
- The coherent longitudinal grain and contact-shadow textures are generated by Canvas at runtime. The fallback is an original Canvas 2D drawing. No external images, models, audio, HDRIs or image-generation tools are used.
- All audio is native Web Audio synthesis: layered 180/470/820 Hz decays, filtered noise transient, a quiet stereo convolution reverb with a generated decaying-noise impulse, low-passed ambience and detuned drone.
- The woodcut mark and sound/ambience icons are original inline SVG paths; controls and type use authored CSS.
