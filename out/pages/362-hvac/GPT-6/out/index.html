FAQ

Straight answers.

How quickly can you get to me?

Timing depends on location, the problem and actual provider availability. This demo does not dispatch technicians.

Do you give a price before starting?

The quote planner shows example starting prices. An on-site diagnosis determines the final scope and price.

What brands do you work on?

Explore service for Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman and Mitsubishi. Actual compatibility requires a technician’s assessment.

Is your work guaranteed?

Any warranty or financing terms must be provided in writing by a real contractor. This concept issues no warranties.

Do I need to be home?

A first visit usually includes a walkthrough and a discussion of options. Arrange access directly with your provider.
