01 PRODUCT THINKING · 2 MIN READBefore the first wireframe

A wireframe can answer where a button goes. It cannot tell you whether anyone needs the button. Start by writing down the behavior you want to understand, who experiences it, and what you still do not know.

Try this: replace “Would you use this?” with “Tell me about the last time you tried to do this.” Specific stories reveal constraints that hypothetical answers hide.

02 DESIGN SYSTEMS · 2 MIN READA component is a conversation

A reusable component records a decision. Document the problem it solves, the situations it supports, and the trade-offs it makes. A button without usage guidance is only half a tool.

Try this: choose one component in your current work. Write a “use when” and an “avoid when” rule. Share both with the next person who uses it.
