GOOD QUESTIONS.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Do I need experience to join?

Absolutely not. Boxing Fundamentals starts with stance, footwork and confidence. Our coaches welcome total beginners.

What equipment do I need?

Just yourself for your first trial. Gloves (8oz–16oz), wraps and pads are available to borrow. Personal protective items are your own; ask your coach.

Is there parking?

Street parking nearby; Hackney Wick Overground is three minutes away on foot. Buses 26, 30 and 388 stop nearby.

Do you run junior classes?

Ages 9–12: Saturday 9am. Ages 13–17: Sunday 2pm. Separate from the adult timetable, taught by Marcus. Contact the gym about junior membership.
