HOW IT WORKS

A lesson is only one
part of the progress.

A simple four-part rhythm keeps student, tutor, and family aligned.

  1. 01

    Free intro

    A 20-minute conversation about current work, goals, and fit.

  2. 02

    Personal starting plan

    Mara Ellison identifies two priorities and agrees on a manageable weekly rhythm.

  3. 03

    Focused 60-minute session

    Work through one concept, guided examples, and increasingly independent practice.

  4. 04

    Short weekly recap

    Receive what clicked, what needs another look, and one concrete practice priority.

YOUR TUTOR, FROM FIRST CALL TO FINAL SESSION

Clear explanations,
without the performance.

“I start by finding the exact step where a problem stops making sense. Then we rebuild it with plain language, a visual example, and practice that gradually removes my help.”

Sessions are calm and focused. You leave with a short recap, one priority for the week, and practice matched to what happened in the lesson.
