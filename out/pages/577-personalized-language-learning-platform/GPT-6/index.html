How is my learning path personalized?

Your level, goal, and focus select a transparent, preset sequence of English lessons. This is deterministic assistance running on your device, not external AI or inferred proficiency.

Can I use this as a secure online account?

This is a static local learning workspace, not an authenticated service. There are no passwords, online accounts, external AI requests, or server storage. Avoid entering sensitive information. Clearing browser data removes your progress.

Are there live exchanges, referrals, or developer APIs?

Not in this homepage edition. Forums, public success stories, referral rewards, third-party APIs, and real meetups need a backend. The journal and practice challenge here work locally. Audio uses your browser's optional English speech voice.
