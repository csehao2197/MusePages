A FEW THINGS, BEFORE YOU LEAP

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A transparent look at the proposed course.

Do I need prior coding experience?

The proposed course begins with absolute basics and builds progressively. The six mini previews here are introductory examples, not a complete zero-to-professional education.

How much time should I dedicate per week?

The sample plan suggests 15–20 hours weekly: 3 hours of live sessions plus 12–17 hours of practice and projects. Actual learning pace varies; no outcome is guaranteed.

What if I fall behind?

The concept proposes recordings and a free move to a later cohort. No operating course, recording library or real transfer policy exists in this static demo.

Do I get a certificate?

A verified completion certificate is a proposed benefit, not an accredited qualification. This demo issues no credential, identity verification or LinkedIn certification.

What happens after the bootcamp?

Lifetime course, community and job-board access plus optional career coaching are concepts. No coaching, employment service or permanent access contract is currently offered.

Is there a refund policy?

The fictional offer proposes a full refund within 30 days, no questions asked. No real payment is accepted and no actual refund entitlement or contract is created here.
