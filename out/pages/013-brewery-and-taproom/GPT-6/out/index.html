INDEPENDENT SINCE THE FIRST BATCH

THREE FRIENDS.
ONE OBSESSION.

Iron Ridge started in 2022 in a garage with a 10-gallon homebrew rig and a questionable amount of ambition. Four years later, we're brewing 5,000 barrels a year in a converted riverside warehouse with views of the Blue Ridge Mountains.

We still brew every batch ourselves. We still taste every beer before it hits the tap. And we still argue about hop schedules.

An imagined brewery story, not a claim about a real business.
