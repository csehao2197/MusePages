BEFORE YOUR FIRST WAVE

GOOD QUESTIONS.

Do I need experience?

Beginner sessions start with paddling, pop-ups and white water. A real instructor must confirm suitability and swimming requirements.

What if the weather changes?

Actual lessons depend on safe conditions. This demo makes no cancellation or rescheduling promises; agree terms with the real school.

Can I bring a group?

Example capacities are six for Beginner, four for Intermediate and two for Advanced. The planner enforces these limits.
