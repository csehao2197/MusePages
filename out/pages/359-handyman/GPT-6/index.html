An honest list

Good at plenty.
Clear about limits.

Work is accepted only within the operator's competence. Regulated or specialist work belongs with the right professional.

What we do

  • Carpentry and joinery repairs
  • Furniture assembly
  • Shelving, mounting and hanging
  • Basic plumbing: taps, traps and seals
  • Like-for-like fittings, where qualified
  • Filling, sanding and small plaster repairs
  • Locks, handles and hinges
  • Blinds, poles and rails
  • Small tiling and grout renewal
  • Fence panel and gate repairs

What we leave to specialists

  • Gas work: use a Gas Safe engineer
  • Notifiable electrical work, new circuits, consumer units and bathroom zones
  • Structural work and load-bearing walls
  • Roof work above single-storey height
  • Asbestos, in any form

No gas, electrical, structural or asbestos qualification is claimed by this concept.
