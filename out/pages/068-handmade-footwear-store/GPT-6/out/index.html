Contact information
Can I change a design?

You can prepare a request, but every edit needs review. Some changes cost more and some may not be feasible. Adding a note is not approval.

How long will my pair take?

These are made-on-demand concepts. A real seller must confirm production timing and delivery before payment; this demo has no dispatch promise.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

The source brief describes nationwide Nigerian delivery, paid by the buyer. Pickup, dispatch rider or waybill arrangements and fees need seller confirmation. No delivery service is connected here.

What if my pair isn't right?

The brief describes case-by-case returns and exchanges, especially for business-originated issues. Read the sample returns summary and agree on final terms with the real seller.

Can I send a gift?

Request packaging and a greeting card in the draft basket. Packaging availability and extra costs are subject to approval. Nothing is sent to a recipient from this demo.

Can I pay here?

No. Paystack and checkout are not connected. You can download a local draft, but no order, payment or stock reservation is created.
