    Still have questions?
    How accurate is the online estimate?

    Our algorithm uses recent comparable sales, tax records, and market trends to provide an estimate within 5-8% of actual market value. For a precise valuation, we recommend a professional CMA.

    Original concept claim, not verified accuracy; this demo runs no valuation algorithm.
    Do I have to be selling to get an estimate?

    Not at all. Many homeowners check their home value annually for financial planning, refinancing, or curiosity. There's zero obligation.

    What is a CMA?

    A Comparative Market Analysis is a detailed report prepared by a licensed real estate agent. It compares your home to similar recently sold properties and accounts for unique features, condition, and local market trends.

    How is this different from Zillow's Zestimate?

    We use local MLS data and agent expertise specific to your neighborhood. Our agents live and work in these communities, providing context that national algorithms miss.

    Original positioning statement; no MLS connection or verified agents are available in this static concept.
    Is my personal information safe?

    Absolutely. We never sell your information to third parties. Your data is used solely to provide your home valuation and connect you with a local Keystone agent if you request it.
