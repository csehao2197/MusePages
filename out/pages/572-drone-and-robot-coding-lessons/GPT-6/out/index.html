    EXPLORER HANDBOOK

    Your first mission.

    01 / Pilot a drone

    Choose any mission. Tap directional controls or focus the grid and use arrow keys. Collect every gold star, avoid solid blocks, then reach the green landing pad. Bumps do not move you.

    02 / Think like a programmer

    Forward moves one square in the robot's current facing direction. Left and Right rotate 90 degrees without moving. Queue up to 24 commands, then run. Execution always starts at the mission's starting position. A collision stops the program. Edit and try again.

    03 / The supply run

    Robot mission 6 requires the cyan supply crate before reaching the landing pad. Watch the robot's direction arrow and plan the order of your route.

    A safe place to experiment

    All movement is a bounded grid simulation in this browser. Nothing executes on or connects to real drones, robots, hardware, a backend, or a code service. Completion is stored locally when browser storage is available. You can explore any mission.
