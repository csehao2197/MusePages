01 — About

The space between design and engineering is where I do my best work.

I grew up sketching typefaces and taking apart my parents' radio, so becoming a frontend engineer felt less like a career choice than an inevitability. For eight years I've worked at the seam where design intent meets production code, at agencies, a commerce scale-up and now a product company with nine teams.

What drives me is the moment an interface disappears and people simply get things done. That takes rigor that rarely shows: semantic markup, careful focus order, performance budgets and motion that guides rather than decorates.

My approach is collaborative and systematic. I prototype early with designers, write things down, and build components so teams can move quickly without breaking the details.

“Good interfaces are quiet. They don't ask for attention — they give it back.”
