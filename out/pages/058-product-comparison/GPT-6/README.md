# TechVault

- Input: `data\prompts\058-product-comparison\058-product-comparison.md` and `src\workflows\AI Agent\DESIGN.md`.
- Model: `gpt-6.1-sol`; authorized directory `GPT-6`. Type: static HTML/CSS/JavaScript.
- Source: this directory. Standalone delivery: `out\index.html` and its local assets; public mapping: `pages/product-comparison/GPT-6/index.html`. Publishing remains a separate authorized operation.
- Features: two/three-model editor, numeric best-value markers, differences/category filters, synchronized mobile table/product selector, compressed sticky header, color-aware cart snapshots with bounded quantities, wishlist, catalog search, example reviews, FAQ, related carousel, local email validation and stateful share URLs.
- Run: serve `out\` using an existing static server. Check `node --check app.js`; `verify.mjs` exports browser interaction assertions for the session checker. The checker validates five widths, subpath resources, desktop/mobile screenshots and a real 1200×900 preview; `.checks\validation.json` records actual results.
- Scope: prices, discounts, ratings, counts, editorial scores and quotes are illustrative. Cart, wishlist and email checks are memory-only; refresh clears shopping data. Shared URLs preserve only comparison settings. Checkout, accounts, fulfillment, stock and newsletters are local demonstrations. No environment variables or backend.
- Accuracy: explicitly distinguishes Sony XM5 (2022), first-generation Bose Ultra (2023), and Lightning AirPods Max (2020). Corrects Sony water rating, folding and Bluetooth, Apple colors/cable, and Bose weight. Numeric Best markers cover only price, battery and weight. Uncited driver diameters and uniform warranty claims are replaced with transparent notes. LDAC is described as lossy; subjective chart scores are examples, not tests.
- Sources: on-page official Sony help guide, Bose first-generation product page and Apple support article `111858`. Sony guide and Bose page were read; Apple support content was available through its indexed official result, while direct requests returned HTTP 429. Bose weight uses its published 0.56lb (~254g). Confirm current regional terms, firmware and exact variants with manufacturers.
- Assets: original SVG headphone/earbud concepts replace product photography and are labeled as non-color-accurate illustrations. Inter is locally hosted from the official rsms/inter repository under the included OFL. Lucide paths are extracted from the existing dependency with its included license; social links use generic camera/video symbols. Native controls keep this dependency-free static page simple.
- Limitations: no hands-on audio testing, live pricing or verified buyers. The seven-item catalog is fixed; related-product specifications are not verified. Refresh-safe comparison settings require a copied share URL. Manufacturer links leave this site. No build, installation or deployment is part of this result.
