Which headphones have the best noise cancellation?

All three offer active noise cancellation. The XM5 uses Sony’s QN1 and V1 processors with eight microphones, not the V2 processor cited in the brief. We have not performed a comparative lab test; fit, firmware and your environment affect the result.

Are these headphones good for working out?

None of the compared models is presented here as water- or sweat-resistant. In particular, the WH-1000XM5 is not IPX4 rated: Sony explicitly warns that the headset is not waterproof. Choose a suitably rated sports model for wet or sweaty workouts.

Can I use these headphones wired?

Sony includes a 3.5mm audio cable. Bose includes a 3.5mm-to-2.5mm cable and must stay powered on. The Lightning AirPods Max requires Apple’s compatible Lightning-to-3.5mm audio cable, sold separately; a generic adapter is not equivalent.

Which headphones are best for phone calls?

Each model has microphones for calls. Bose describes adaptive voice pickup, Sony offers beamforming and noise reduction, and Apple uses beamforming microphones. We have not independently ranked call quality. Test in your own calling environment if possible.

Do any of these support lossless wireless audio?

The compared modes do not establish bit-perfect lossless Bluetooth playback. Sony’s LDAC can use a higher bitrate on compatible sources but remains lossy. Codec availability depends on the device and software. These Lightning AirPods Max are distinct from later USB-C models with different wired audio features.
