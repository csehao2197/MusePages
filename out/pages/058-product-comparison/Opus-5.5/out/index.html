Common Questions

Which headphones have the best noise cancellation?

In our testing, the Sony WH-1000XM5 edges out the competition with its Integrated Processor V2 and 8 microphones, especially on low-frequency engine and train rumble. The Bose is a very close second.

Are these headphones good for working out?

Only the Sony WH-1000XM5 has IPX4 water/sweat resistance, so it's the only one we'd recommend for light workouts. Over-ear designs still get warm during intense exercise.

Can I use these headphones wired?

Yes — Sony and Bose include 3.5mm cables. Apple uses Lightning, so you'll need a separate Lightning-to-3.5mm adapter cable for analog sources.

Which headphones are best for phone calls?

The Bose QuietComfort Ultra leads in call quality, isolating your voice well in noisy streets. Sony is good indoors but less consistent outside.

Do any of these support lossless audio?

The Sony XM5 supports LDAC for near-lossless streaming from compatible Android devices. Bose supports aptX Adaptive on supported phones; AirPods Max is limited to AAC over Bluetooth.
