A NOTE FOR NEW COURIERS

There is no wrong turn.

Welcome to Amberwick Isle, an autumn woodland island for players who enjoy quiet exploration.

  1. Pick up the letter at the Post House with E or the action button.
  2. Walk with W A S D, arrow keys, or the touch arrows. “Follow trail” walks the route for you; any movement cancels it.
  3. Visit Wind, River, and Mountain shrines in any order. Interact nearby to collect each stamp.
  4. With all three stamps, follow the north trail and cross the open temple gate to deliver your letter.

No timer. No combat. Stamps and sound preference stay on this device; each visit starts safely at the Post House.
