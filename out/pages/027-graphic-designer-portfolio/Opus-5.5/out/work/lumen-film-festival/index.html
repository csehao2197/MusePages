Lumen Film Festival 2026

A light-leak campaign system for a ten-day independent film festival across posters, trailers and social.

The challenge

The festival needed one campaign idea that could be produced in-house by a two-person comms team for 60+ screenings, from a 48-sheet poster to a 15-second story.

Audience & constraints

Local film-goers aged 20–45, students and visiting industry guests.

  • Templates editable in common tools by non-designers
  • High contrast for night-time outdoor posters
  • Programme changes up to a week before print

Creative direction

Light through a projector gate: the wordmark is sliced by diagonal beams, and each programme strand gets its own beam angle so the system feels varied without new artwork.

Key decisions

  1. A single beam grid at 18° drives every layout
  2. Yellow on black for maximum night-time contrast; strand colours only in beams
  3. Film titles always set in the same weight and size step
