Field Notes Quarterly

A redesign of an independent nature magazine around a flexible grid that makes long reads easier to follow.

The challenge

Readers loved the writing but found long features hard to navigate. The small team needed templates that kept issues consistent without a full-time art director.

Audience & constraints

Subscribers who read in long sittings, plus newsstand browsers judging the cover in seconds.

  • Same page count and paper stock
  • Templates usable by a part-time production editor
  • Illustrations commissioned per issue in different styles

Creative direction

A field-guide feel: generous margins for notes, a three-column body grid with a hanging caption column, and ochre used only for navigation.

Key decisions

  1. Running heads double as a table-of-contents locator
  2. Pull quotes set at a fixed position so long reads keep their rhythm
  3. Covers use one illustration and one masthead size — nothing else
