Our story

Where Hair
Meets Art

Atelier Blanche was born from a simple conviction: that your hair should make you feel like yourself, only more. We opened our doors in the heart of the city with a small team of exceptional stylists and a shared obsession with craft.

Every appointment here is deliberate. We don't rush. We don't over-book. We listen, we create, and work with you towards a result that feels personal.

“Hair is the most intimate form of personal expression. We take that seriously.”
