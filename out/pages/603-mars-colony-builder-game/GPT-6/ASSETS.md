# Assets and attribution

All crater terrain, boulders, four structures, selection, dust, storm veil, build glyphs and favicon are original code-drawn canvas/inline SVG art using the prompt's geometry and palette. No external image was used. No downloaded textures, photographs, video, sprites, or audio files are included.

Five utility icons only — `CircleHelp` (installed alias `CircleQuestionMark`), `Volume2`, `VolumeX`, `Undo2`, and `X` — were rendered from the locally installed [`lucide-react`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/lucide-react) package, by Lucide Icons and Contributors, ISC. The complete package notice, including applicable Feather/Cole Bemis MIT attribution, is `licenses\lucide-LICENSE.txt`. Build glyphs are original, not Lucide replacements.
