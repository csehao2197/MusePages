All sleeves, records, stamps, logomarks, favicon, SVG paper grain and synthetic audio sketches are original code created for this demo. No photographs, downloaded image files, scanned textures or third-party audio are used.

- `@fontsource/anton`: Anton 400 Latin, SIL OFL 1.1.
- `@fontsource/space-grotesk`: Space Grotesk 400/500/700 Latin, SIL OFL 1.1.
- `@fontsource/space-mono`: Space Mono 400/700 Latin, SIL OFL 1.1.
- `lucide-react`: ISC. Only Disc3, ShoppingBag, Plus, Minus, X, ArrowRight and ArrowUpRight are exported as inline SVG markup from the installed package; `lib\LICENSE` preserves its license.
