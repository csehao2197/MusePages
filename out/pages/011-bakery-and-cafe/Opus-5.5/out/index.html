Our story

From Our Kitchen to Yours

What started as a weekend farmers market stand in 2018 has grown into the neighborhood's favorite gathering spot. We're Elena and Marcus, and we believe great bread takes time — our sourdough starter is older than the shop. Every loaf, pastry, and cup of coffee is made with intention.
