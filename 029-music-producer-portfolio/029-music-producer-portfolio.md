Design: Dark, cinematic, audio-forward. Primary: #8B5CF6 (purple-500 for CTAs, accents, and interactive elements). Background spectrum: #0A0A0F (darkest, base), #0F0F1A (sections), #1A1A2E (cards/surfaces). Text: white (#FFFFFF) for headings, #A1A1AA (zinc-400) for body text, #6B7280 (gray-500) for metadata. Accent glow: purple (#8B5CF6) at 20-30% opacity for hover states, borders, and background effects. Typography: Space Grotesk for headings (bold, modern, geometric), Inter for body text. Cards: 12-16px border-radius, no visible borders by default, purple glow on hover (box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(139,92,246,0.15)). Generous spacing between sections (80-120px). Album art and photos should feel cinematic — consider subtle Ken Burns (slow zoom) effects on hero images.

Responsive: Mobile-first. Hero text scales down (64px name, 16px tagline). Beat grid becomes 1-column. Persistent player simplifies to essential controls. License cards stack vertically with "Most Popular" on top. Discography becomes swipe carousel. About section stacks (photo on top, text below). Contact form goes full-width. All touch targets 44px minimum. Navigation becomes full-screen overlay menu with large text links and social icons at bottom.
